## 0. The one-paragraph spine

A star is the **maximally leg-split** object: massive, frame-bearing, with a clock and a worldline,
its bulk crammed with ordering distinctions (composition, multipoles, the order in which things
happened). It sits deep in the interior of the capacity-interval, far from both framelessness poles.
Collapse drives it onto the **null cone**: the outgoing ordering-leg and the ingoing adjacency-leg
**rotate into coincidence** (relativity of simultaneity run to its limit), a **trapped surface**
forms, and at that surface the bulk **ordering-charge** (the Noether/modular charge of the
causal-time symmetry, `= M` in the bulk) gets **fused** onto a transverse **adjacency-area**
(`S = A/4`, Wald). Fusion is the **first appearance of a modular fixed point**: before collapse no
boost orbit has an invariant surface; after, the marginally-trapped surface is one. The real fusion
locus is **local and present** (the apparent horizon / trapped surface, where the outgoing
ordering-angle first pins to zero expansion), not the **teleological, future** event horizon — Wald's
bifurcation surface is the *stationary limit* the local fixed point relaxes to, so the exact fusion
operator only sharpens *after* fusion is effectively done. Penrose ties it off: **trapped ⇒
singular** is **first fixed point ⇒ total ordering-collapse** — the ordering-angle degenerating on
one surface (horizon) guarantees it degenerating on the whole congruence (singularity). Fusion is
**fast and classical** (focusing, free-fall, `ħ`-free); un-fusion is **slow and quantum**
(evaporation, `t ∝ M³/ħ`). So there is **no Page curve in reverse** — formation **opens** the
coarse/fine entropy gap as a *step* (`A/4` jumps over `S_matter`); evaporation **closes** it as a
*curve*. The asymmetry is the `ħ`-asymmetry, and it is the local steepest-ascent face of the
spine §14 arrow. No-hair is the classical statement of fusion: the legs **pinned** by Wald, the bulk
ordering distinctions **hidden behind the area**.

Fusion is the event that makes `S_coarse` **leap over** `S_fine`. The fine-grained content is not

## 3. Fixed-point nucleation: apparent horizon vs event horizon

The spine's deepest weld (§10–§11): the entropy surface is the **fixed point** of the ordering
symmetry — the boost (Killing) fixed point when stationary, the replica `Z_n` fixed point (QES) when
dynamical. Both are faces of modular flow; its fixed point is **where the ordering-charge fuses to
the adjacency-area**.

So formation has a crisp signature: it is the **first appearance of such a fixed point**.

```text
BEFORE collapse:
  no Killing horizon, no bifurcation surface
  every ordering-rotation (boost-analog) orbit is free — NO invariant transverse surface
  the ordering-angle has nowhere to degenerate; legs stay split everywhere

AFTER fixed-point nucleation:
  a marginally-trapped surface exists: outgoing null expansion θ_(+) = 0
  the ordering-rotation now LEAVES A SURFACE INVARIANT — the first fixed point
  on it the ordering-angle has zero proper extent (spine §10 cigar tip)
```

**Which surface is the real fusion locus?** Two candidates, and they are not the same object:

```text
EVENT horizon:
  teleological — defined by the global future (rays that never reach null infinity)
  can grow through empty space BEFORE matter arrives ("knows the future")
  is a Killing/bifurcation surface only ASYMPTOTICALLY, after ringdown
  → this is where Wald is EXACT, i.e. where fusion is already COMPLETE

APPARENT horizon / trapped surface:
  local, present-time — outermost θ_(+) = 0, decidable on a slice
  is the place the ordering-angle FIRST pins to zero expansion
  is the dynamical (found, not given) fixed point — the QES family, not the Killing one
```

In MGR terms fusion is a **local, present event** — a charge fusing onto a *local* transverse area —
so the real fusion locus is the **apparent horizon / trapped surface**, the dynamical fixed point
that has to be **found** (spine §11: "selector = the search for the modular fixed point when there is
no Killing one"). The event horizon is the **teleological smoothing**: the surface the local fixed
point *relaxes to* once the geometry rings down and an exact Killing field — hence an exact Wald
bifurcation surface — exists.

This exposes a structural oddity the death-side never had to face:

> **The fusion happens dynamically and locally (apparent horizon, now), but the operator that
> *defines* the fused state (Wald at the bifurcation surface) is only exact in the stationary limit —
> i.e. only once fusion is effectively over. The exact fusion operator is retroactive.**

Tie to the spine's dynamical face: at formation there is no clean replica `Z_n` symmetry to fix in
the Page sense, so the QES is not yet a Page-curve QES. The marginally-trapped surface is the
**classical shadow** of the would-be fixed point — the place the modular flow *will* settle its fixed
point. Collapse is the **nucleation** of that fixed point out of a spacetime that had none.


Read `θ` through the cigar (spine §10): near a horizon the ordering-leg is the **angle** `κτ`,
degenerating to zero proper length at the fixed point. **Focusing is that degeneration run
dynamically along the congruence.** As `θ → 0` you reach the marginally-trapped surface (the first

— the outgoing leg can no longer separate from the ingoing, and the capacity-object is **pinned to
the null cone** (spine §5–§6: "null → legs coincide = frameless").

```text
θ_(+) = 0      :  HORIZON          — ordering-angle pins on ONE surface       (first fixed point)
θ_(+) < 0 all  :  TRAPPED REGION   — even the outgoing leg ingoing-directed   (basin)
θ_(+) → −∞     :  SINGULARITY      — ordering-angle collapses on the WHOLE     (total
                                      congruence; leg-coincidence everywhere    ordering-collapse, b4)
```

Penrose's theorem (trapped surface + NEC + global hyperbolicity ⇒ geodesic incompleteness) then
reads, in this vocabulary:

> **first fixed point ⟹ total ordering-collapse.**

The horizon (one degenerate ordering-angle) **guarantees** the singularity (all of them). So the two
b4 sites — horizon and singularity — are not independent: fusion (horizon) and ordering-collapse
(singularity) are bound by the focusing theorem. Restated against the spine's static result:

```text

                      congruence  → the singularity is the fixed point eating the trapped region
```

The area law is the **fixed-point-at-rest**; the singularity is the **fixed-point-running-to-
completion**. Same degeneration, evaluated on a surface vs propagated through a region.

## 5. The time-asymmetry: fast classical fuse, slow quantum un-fuse

Birth and death run at wildly different rates.

```text
FUSION  (collapse)     :  free-fall / light-crossing   t_form  ~  GM/c³        ~ M
UN-FUSION (evaporation):  Hawking leak                 t_evap  ~  G²M³/(ħc⁴)   ~ M³/ħ
ratio                  :  t_evap / t_form  ~  (M/M_Pl)²   (≈ 10⁷⁵ for a solar mass)
```

The cause is an `ħ`-asymmetry, and it is clean:

```text
FUSION is CLASSICAL:
  driven by Raychaudhuri focusing under the NEC — pure GR geometry, NO ħ
  attractive gravity is monotone; the ordering-angle is forced to degenerate
  t_form is ħ-independent (survives the ħ → 0 limit)

UN-FUSION is QUANTUM:
  driven by the leg-rotation rate T = κ/2π ~ ħ/M (spine §6); the flux ~ ħ
  t_evap → ∞ as ħ → 0 — there is NO classical un-fusion at all
```

So the same object's life is **classically irreversible at birth and quantum-slow at death**. Fusion
needs no `ħ`; un-fusion is *entirely* an `ħ`-effect. That is why one scales `M` and the other `M³/ħ`:
the `M³` carries the `1/ħ` of the ħ-bridge (b4 §1, §5). Maximal leg-rotation is capped at the Planck
wall (spine §15); minimal is irrelevant to collapse, which is driven by geometry, not rotation.

**Both processes increase total entropy** — there is one arrow, not two. Formation: `A/4` leaps over
`S_matter` (a large coarse-entropy gain). Evaporation: the radiation carries out *more* fine-grained
entropy than the hole held. Map onto the spine §14 traverse:

```text
spine §14:  universe begins FUSED (Big Bang, external/null pole, legs coincident)
            and traverses toward UN-FUSED (de Sitter floor) — the arrow of time

collapse is the LOCAL steepest-ascent of that same arrow:
  a black hole is a local re-fusion — a little patch driven back toward the null pole
  Penrose's Weyl hypothesis: Weyl → 0 at the smooth Big Bang (low grav. entropy),
  Weyl → ∞ at the singularity (high grav. entropy)
  collapse climbs gravitational entropy fast precisely BECAUSE focusing is classical and monotone
```

So the local fast-fuse is not against the cosmic arrow — it **is** the arrow, run at its steepest
where gravity is allowed to focus freely.

## 6. The reverse-Page is a step, not a movie

Now the explicit question: is there a Page curve in reverse?

The honest answer is **no — and the reason is structural, not incidental.** The Page curve is a
*crossing of two competing legs* (spine §12):

```text
S_rad(t) = min( S_ordering↑ , S_adjacency↓ )      PAGE TIME = the crossing (fixed-point jump)
```

It is a curve with a turnover because two legs slowly trade dominance. Formation has **no such slow
competition**. Collapse simply drops the fine-grained matter behind a suddenly-large area:

```text
FORMATION:   S_coarse = A/4  LEAPS over  S_fine = S_matter        — a STEP (gap opens, fast)
LIFE:        gap stays open  (A/4 − S_fine = hidden ordering)     — the lid holds
EVAPORATION: S_fine of radiation climbs to meet falling A/4       — a CURVE (gap closes, slow)
```

```text
        S
        │            ____________  A/4 (coarse)
        │           /            \____
        │   step → |                  \___  Page turnover
        │__________|  ____________________\____  S_fine (radiation)
        │         /  /
        └────────┴──┴──────────────────────────────▶ t
              formation                evaporation
              (gap OPENS, classical)   (gap CLOSES, quantum)
```

The "Page curve in reverse" would require the **closing curve run backwards** — area *growing* while
fine-grained radiation entropy *falls back into* a forming hole, slowly, thermally. That is a **white
hole accreting its own Hawking radiation**, which is *not* the time-reverse of collapse. Two distinct
facts, often conflated:

```text
time-reverse of COLLAPSE     = a white hole EXPLODING a star outward (a valid but unstable GR solution)
time-reverse of EVAPORATION  = a white hole COHERING thermal radiation into a star (measure-zero)
⟹ fusion (collapse) and un-fusion (evaporation) are NOT time-reverses of each other;
   they are BOTH forward-time processes in one black hole's life.
```

This is the cleanest statement of the asymmetry: collapse and evaporation are not a reversible pair
seen from two ends. They are two different forward-time legs, glued at the stationary middle.

**Why is fusion irreversible?** GR itself is time-symmetric (the collapse solution time-reversed is a
legal white hole). The arrow is imposed the same way the spine imposes it in §8:

```text
spine §8 Silk damping:  reversible leg-rotation (phase) decoheres into the irreversible RECORD;
                        the second law made visible as where phase is destroyed → record written

collapse:  infall DECOHERES — the branches that fell in get recorded against the exterior;
           the low-entropy, leg-split initial star is the boundary condition that orients the arrow
           (a near-null, leg-coincident past — the Big Bang — not a thermal one)
```

So fusion is irreversible for the **same reason Silk damping is**: a record is written, phase is
destroyed, and the arrow is anchored by a low-entropy (maximally leg-split) past. The white-hole
reverse is forbidden not by the field equations but by the record/decoherence asymmetry — exactly the
spine's §8 mechanism, now applied to the formation edge.

> **There is no reverse Page movie because fusion is a decoherence STEP (record written, classical,
> fast), while un-fusion is a rotation CURVE (phase leaked out, quantum, slow). The gap opens by
> recording and closes by radiating.**

## 7. No-hair: fusion hides the ordering distinctions behind the area

The star carried enormous bulk structure. The stationary black hole carries `M`, `J`, `Q` and
nothing else. No-hair is the **classical** statement of what fusion does to the ordering distinctions.

```text
STAR:   ordering distinctions = composition, layering, multipole tower, the order of its history
        — all frame-bearing, all "legs split" content

FUSION (Wald pins the legs):
        only the conserved Noether labels survive as visible charge:  M (and J, Q)
        every other distinction is screened — inaccessible from outside the area

BLACK HOLE:  no hair = the ordering-charge distinctions HIDDEN BEHIND the adjacency-area
```

In the legs language: Wald identifies `charge|_bifurcation = A/4`, so from outside, **only the total
ordering-charge is legible**; the internal ordering-structure that made it up is folded behind the
transverse area (the cigar's spectator `(y,z)`, spine §10). No-hair is **fusion as a screen** — the
maximal-hiding configuration.

This is the exact dual of the death-side §5 of the spine:

```text
FORMATION:    no-hair  = fusion HIDES the ordering distinctions behind the area   (maximal hiding)
EVAPORATION:  Page curve = un-fusion UN-HIDES them into the radiation              (the un-hiding movie)
```

No-hair is not loss; it is concealment. The distinctions are pinned, not destroyed (unitary spine
commitment). The could-fail is whether the concealment is *perfect*: classical no-hair is exact, but
**soft / quantum hair** would mean the area leaks a faint imprint of what fused — a non-zero residual
that the spine's S2 (ordering-dominated, irreducible-to-area) would need at the formation edge.
