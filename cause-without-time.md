# What Could "Cause Without Time" Mean? — A Mereological Decomposition from the Photon

*Sree Kotay — working draft, June 2026.*

*Companion documents: `gravity-information-adjacency.md` (the four-momentum application) and `record-vs-constitution.md` (working derivations); see §1.5 for how refinements propagate between them.*

## Abstract

General relativity is held fixed as ground truth throughout: nothing here modifies, extends, or quantizes GR. The paper instead addresses a class of questions on which GR's mathematics is silent — mereological questions: what composes cause, time, information, space, and gravity-as-structure. The method begins at a degenerate corner GR itself exhibits. Along a null ray dτ = 0, yet cone-direction and interventional effect remain intact: the photon is causal with no proper time, so whatever cause is, temporal flow is not a constituent of it. Read at this corner, cause unfuses into three unitless roles — ordering (invariant cone-direction), influence (effect-substrate), and flow (proper-time accrual along a timelike worldline) — two exhibited, one stripped. The paper's single wager is to treat these roles as composable constituents and to project, under GR held fixed, what must then be true. Every step is graded: forced (exactly one reading survives) or a bet (a projection into a GR-silence, carrying a stated failure condition). Principal results: a record/constitution distinction under which information is frame-relative in the same sense as proper time and particle content (witnessed at Unruh for rendering; full observer-free constitution remains an open bet); compositions for time, space, and the invariant speed (c as adjacency-per-flow, the shared ordering-spine cancelling); de-conflations of standard puzzle-narrations (tunneling "superluminality," delayed-choice "retrocausation") with every standard number left standing; and four priced bets (PB-1–PB-4), each with its falsifier stated. Failed checks and downgrades are retained in a corrections log. Uniform success would be the failure signal.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The question, and GR held fixed

GR is mathematically correct and its predictive compression is rightly heralded. But GR is silent on certain mereological questions, and the photon in particular is both elegant and vexing: it traverses, orders, and acts, while accruing no proper time at all. The standard response is to treat this as a curiosity of the null limit. This paper treats it instead as data — an exhibited fused composition, decomposed and performed by GR's own mathematics, of a notion (cause) that the theory uses but never anatomizes.

The framework therefore takes the mathematics and predictive compression of GR as correct a priori, and attempts to rigorously project into the questions on which it is silent: what composes cause, time, information, space, gravity-as-structure? The stance is following GR further, not refuting it. GR is sound; where it is silent or bundles roles into one object (metric, source), that is read as a mereological gap — a role not yet separated — not a foundational gap. The default on a GR silence or bundle is to separate the role, never to supply missing physics. The working expectation is that GR's regularities (e.g. a bound like c on a role-exchange) extend to separated roles.

### 1.2 Method

The method: from the photon under GR-fixed, unfuse exactly three unitless component-roles (no more, no fewer) — the two the corner exhibits and the one it strips. Treat those roles as composable constituents for real: this realism is the paper's declared wager, not a forced step. Then project what **must** be true under GR's mathematics if the roles compose — the compositions, the mandatory questions (e.g. the dual cell the photon's profile forces), and the bounds. The realism is paid for in falsifiable bets; the bets are the realism's price, and their failure conditions are stated where they are made.

The mereological instrument is completeness — compose / missing / extra — not taxonomy:

- **compose** — parts present.
- **missing** — a part needed but absent.
- **extra** — present but unaccounted.

A composition holds only with no missing and no extra — the parts determine the whole, nothing left free. Parts merely present is consistency, not composition.

**Forced** is the gate the method runs under: a move carries weight only if no alternative reading survives once GR is held fixed as ground-truth math — exactly one reading live. If two or more remain, the move is chosen, not forced (manufacture) — a move declined under this methodology. The compose / missing / extra checks test whether a decomposition is complete; forced tests whether the step was necessary. Completeness can pass and still be taxonomy — only forced makes it carry weight.

Import and proscription run under the same gate. Import is licensed by forced-from-ground + completeness, never by being GR-native (a non-GR thing imports if it derives forced from GR + method ground and enters complete; only the unforced is excluded). A presumed proscription — reading a GR-silence as a prohibition — discharges as a bet, not a fact. Outside-GR is not disqualifying; bare exclusion is not a move.

The method and measures (compose / missing / extra) are tools for reducing epistemic incompleteness, not a claim that any decomposition here is complete or its parts irreducible.

### 1.3 Claims and non-claims

What the paper claims: a set of role-separations read off GR's own corners; compositions built from them (time, space, c) graded individually; contact-grade readings of standard phenomena in which every standard number is left standing and only the narration changes; and four bets — projections into GR-silences — each priced with a falsifier. What it does not claim: no new dynamics (the field equations and couplings are imported or out by kind); no derivation of magnitudes as GR is scale-free (dimensional counts are presently open, couplings are out by kind); no substance-ontology (the components are roles, not stuff — §2); and no adjudication among interpretations of quantum mechanics beyond the specific role-content checked. Where the framework's reading merely re-describes, the re-description is labeled as carrying no weight (§1.5).

### 1.4 Relation to existing work

Individuation by role is fundamental physics' default mode, not an exotic posture: GR ontologizes gravity as laying-out structure rather than substance; Wigner's classification individuates particles as representation-profiles; gauge fields enter as connection coefficients, individuated by how they transform and couple. The mathematics has no further slot for what anything is beyond its role-profile. What is distinctive here is the procedure — individuation by corner-degeneration (what survives when GR's own math strips a constituent to zero) rather than by invariance — and the grading discipline attached to it. Adjacent programs are noted rather than adjudicated: relational quantum mechanics and the thermal-time program operate at the same constitution and flow seams (a consistency run against Frauchiger–Renner-type pressure is queued in the working document); the quantum-Darwinism criteria literature is the live testing ground for the record/constitution boundary (reduced and tracked in the working document). Where the framework's readings collide with named formalisms, the collisions are run as checks and logged, in both directions.

### 1.5 Epistemic grading (labels — applied per claim, not per object)

- **definition** — a stipulation or composition (a carve). Judged by carve-quality, not truth; carries **no** confirmatory weight - that work follows. Most of the framework's construction is definitions (the compositions, the roles, the perimeter).
- **label** — inert re-description of one thing in the framework's vocabulary. No weight; rare.
- **witness** — phenomenon run through compose/missing/extra; could have failed mereologically, didn't (or failed correctly). Carries confirmatory weight.
- **probe** — interior/non-extremal reading that **bounds** a term without reaching its boundary. Carries bounding weight only.
- **echo** — independent structure our reading maps onto; **silent** (permits, doesn't force). Carries **no** weight; consistent-with, not evidence.
- **clarifier** — a contact-grade verdict: the framework's reading re-describes or de-conflates a known result correctly, predicting no new number (the numbers are owned by the standard formalism). Confirms consistency, carries no predictive weight. Distinct from echo (a clarifier is the framework's own reading checked against a phenomenon; an echo is an independent structure that happens to permit the reading).
- **failed floor** — attempted reduction that didn't deliver; logged as negative.
- **parked texture** — not precluded, not suggested; demoted from evidence, live to revisit; do-not-hunt where hunting is the fitting trap.

**Discipline**: only witness confirms; i.e. probe bounds; echo / failed-floor / parked carry no confirmatory weight and may **not** be cited as support. This attempts to keep broad exploration from collapsing into "everything confirms me." An object can hold different labels in different contexts (neutrino = witness mereologically, probe on the s-axis).

**Status-scope**: this doc asserts only what its own derivations establish; its status lines (open / owed / parked) are relative to that basis. Refinements derived in the working doc (record-vs-constitution.md) supersede them only on promotion, after a cold read in a later session; until then, the working doc holds the set's current state of those items, and this doc's statuses are correct at their own level, not stale.

Descriptive move-names (excavation, reflection, quant-composition) name **how** a move was made or its shape; they are not weight-classes and never substitute for an epistemic slot. Every claim carries a slot from the list above (definition / label / witness / probe / echo / failed-floor / parked) regardless of how it was reached. A composition — including a quant-composition — is a definition (zero confirmatory weight).

## 2. Components

These are role-names, not substance-claims. Mereology gives the parts and their separability; it is silent on what the parts are made of. "Component" therefore means separable role, not object — which is why entries of different ontological grade (a role, a substrate, a bookkeeping projection, a dimensionful primitive) sit together in one list, sorted by role rather than by kind of being.

The entries are not all of one grade, and the grading is the point. The framework's own carve yields exactly **three roles — ordering, influence, and flow** (§1.2: the two the photon corner exhibits, the one it strips). These are the specific new components: cause, time, and information compose from them, and the bets price them. The remaining entries support the three and arrive differently. **Adjacency** is a role of the same kind (unitless, laying-out) but of different provenance — imported whole from GR's metric rather than unfused at a corner: the spatial peer of flow, needed for space and c, named late. **Relatedness** is the substrate all four presuppose. **Energy-momentum** is the dimensionful primitive, imported as-is. So the count is three derived roles, plus one imported role, a substrate, and a primitive — not four undifferentiated roles; derived-at-corner versus imported is the import discipline of §1.2, holding at the roster itself. Some entries were used pervasively before being listed — energy-momentum, adjacency, signature, frame — because GR bundles them into its metric, source, and tetrad; they were named late. Naming a bundled role is the mereological move: the entries below are GR's roles separated, plus influence, which is separated at the QT seam.

**The three derived roles (the defusion):**

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| ordering | Invariant cone-direction — **not** the interval or duration between events. |
| influence | Effect-substrate: that in virtue of which one relatum's state constrains another's. Strictly this — not force, push, transfer, or agency. Separable from ordering and flow; its floor is narrowed (AB: influence ≠ force), with the what-instead open. |
| flow | The flow-bearing worldline process: proper-time accrual along a timelike worldline. Directed, single-track; the **temporal** laying-out of relata. ("Becoming" is a gloss on the component, not its physical content — physics reads only the magnitude; see below.) Clock is its magnitude projection (bookkeeping). |

**Relatedness, confirmed in passing.** The radiation-reaction result (§11.7) — "self-force" survives as two-place through the field — reads as confirmation that influence needs two related places.

**Time and space as declared peers.** Space = ordering + adjacency, paralleling time = ordering + flow, with the proportion flow : clock : energy :: adjacency : separation-magnitude : momentum. The signature (−+++) is the flow/adjacency split — one timelike laying-out, three spacelike. Presently the dimensional counts (1 timelike, 3 spacelike) are imported, not composed: provisionally open to later derivation, **not** brute by kind (unlike the coupling, which is out by kind, as the metric-on-the-space). There are thus two grades of "out": by-kind (couplings — never a component) and presently (counts — unfinished, possibly closable). [Open: the c/s scheme with space as named peer — c = adjacency per flow.]

**Influence is encountered, not inferred.** Influence is not a leftover. It is met positively, as dependence — effect-substrate; the subtraction at the corners only proves the role separable from ordering and flow, it does not define it.

**The dimensionful primitive and its contingency.** Declaring the dimensionful primitive carries a contingency: the framework stands on SR/GR kinematics. It adopts SR/GR's dimensionful primitive (energy-momentum, via E=mc²); it does not adopt SR/GR's dynamical law (the field equations, G_N). Mass is the rest-frame face (energy at p=0); energy-momentum is the general, not-at-rest primitive. The proton case — mass dominated by binding energy — is a correspondence (binding ↔ influence-content), not a composition of mass; mass remains a face of the declared primitive, not a derivation from the roles.

**Mass as flow-projection** (emerges from reading space and gravity together; it completes the relationship, it does not derive the magnitude). Momentum is the conjugate of adjacency (space = ordering + adjacency); energy is the conjugate of flow (time = ordering + flow). Rest-mass — energy at p=0 — is therefore energy-momentum lying purely in the flow-conjugate face, with the adjacency-conjugate (momentum) zeroed: mass is the flow-projection (temporal alignment) of energy-momentum, and momentum is its adjacency-projection (spatial alignment). E=mc² (rest, pure flow) and E=pc (massless, pure adjacency) are the two limit-projections; intermediate systems split across both. The magnitude relation (m²c⁴) is imported (the dispersion relation); the role-alignment (mass↔flow, momentum↔adjacency) is the framework's reading. This makes one quantity — energy-momentum — projected onto the two laying-out roles, and accounts in one move for three otherwise-separate facts: rest-mass anchors flow (having mass = energy-momentum aligned with flow = proper time accrues); massless means flow→0 (energy-momentum all in the adjacency face); and gravity couples to energy-momentum, not rest-mass (it couples to both faces; rest-mass is only the flow-face slice). Not a truth-claim — a relationship the mereology completes.

**The law.** The law (couplings: G_N) is the metric on this space, not a region in it. Cell-occupancy is the open work; the structure carries no confirmatory weight.

**Flow is physics-silent beyond its magnitude.** Physics reads only the clock (magnitude) representation of flow, never a "becoming" gloss. This is confirmed at the hardest case: the Page curve is a static S(τ) — a magnitude — plus ordering (the curve's orientation). So "information = cause + flow" enters physically as cause + nonzero clock-magnitude.

**Flow as frame phenomenon (write, not read).** Proper time accrues along a timelike worldline — frame-intrinsic, observer-independent. "Write" names this process: the act with no actor (the worldline accrues; no agent is involved). "Read" smuggles an observer. This grounds PB-4 — information constituted by frame-process, not observer (see §16, Predictions). The write's mechanism, its strict scope, and the located result it yields are given in §4.

**Bookkeeping projections** — transformable labels of phenomena already in the set (not demotions; changes of representation):

- clock ↔ flow: mutual projections (clock is the magnitude representation of flow); proper-time is the composition (ordering + flow), not a parent dimension. (Name and shape open — clock/flow may not be the right cut.)
- orientation: the projection of ordering (global orientability).
- separation-magnitude: the magnitude of adjacency (proper distance / area / volume), as clock is the magnitude of flow. The component behind it is adjacency. Time-dilation (clock = flow-magnitude) and length-dilation (separation-magnitude = adjacency-magnitude) are the two faces of metric scale, symmetric.
- reachability = connection + separation — derived.
- separation = interval-type (timelike / null / spacelike): geometry, not a component. Hence reachability is derived and orientation is a projection.

## 3. Compositions

Each composition below is a definition — a carve, judged by carve-quality, not truth, and carrying no confirmatory weight (§1.5). They are written as role-sums; "+" means the roles are jointly present and determine the whole, with no missing part and no extra (§1.2). The compositions share one skeleton — ordering — and differ in what they add to it.

**cause = ordering + influence.** No clock, no flow; scale-robust; no remainder. This is the seed composition, read off the photon corner: what survives when flow is stripped.

**time = ordering + flow.** No influence. The clock is fractured by scale (quantum: no time operator; cosmic: a(t)). Flow enters through its clock (magnitude) projection; the process beyond its magnitude is physics-silent (§2).

**space = ordering + adjacency.** The spatial peer of time — adjacency (extension / nextness) where time has flow (the worldline process). Adjacency enters through its separation-magnitude projection (proper distance / area / volume). This restores a symmetry the framework was missing: it had time-as-composition but no space-as-composition (space was a degenerate corner). "True space" is ordering + adjacency, not a leftover.

The conjugates follow the two laying-out roles: energy is conjugate to flow (time), momentum conjugate to adjacency (space) — the two faces of the energy-momentum primitive. Mass is **not** a third conjugate: it is the invariant magnitude of the four-momentum (its rest value, energy at p=0), not a quantity conjugate to ordering. The four-momentum's two legs are time-momentum — the (ordering+flow)-projection, the timelike part, with magnitude from flow and zero when flow→0 (which is why the photon is massless) — and space-momentum, the (ordering+adjacency)-projection, the spacelike part, ordinary momentum. So energy-momentum = time-momentum + space-momentum, where "+" again means "projects into": the role-decomposition names the two projections but does **not** specify how they combine. The combination law — the quadrature — is imported, and the mass-shell E²=(pc)²+(mc²)² is that imported combination: the quadrature of the two legs, with mass the invariant magnitude. (Photon: pure space-momentum, time-momentum zero, E=|p|c. Rest: pure time-momentum, space-momentum zero, E=mc².)

Ordering has no conjugate quantity, and this is a **category fact**, not a gap. Ordering is the shared spine of both legs — it appears in both time = ordering+flow and space = ordering+adjacency — the axis the legs are projected **along**, not one of the projected legs; asking for its conjugate quantity is a category error, like asking the momentum of a coordinate axis. This is why ordering cancels in c's units: c = space/time = (ordering+adjacency)/(ordering+flow) = adjacency/flow measured along the shared ordering spine — leg over leg, spine cancelling. c is the rate at which the two legs trade along the spine; the photon is the spine made manifest (the null ray, the cone-edge), c saturated. Contrast s = influence/ordering, a ratio measured **against** the spine, with ordering in the denominator and not cancelling — which is why c and s are not exact peers: c is leg/leg along the spine, s is influence against it.

This is decomposed GR, round-tripping: composing the role-projections back gives GR's dispersion relation (the four-momentum, its two legs, the invariant mass) with nothing missing and nothing extra. The decomposition is faithful — a consistency result, not a new claim.

**frame = ordering + flow + adjacency** (= space ∪ time, glued on shared ordering; GR's tetrad, decomposed). The influence-free laying-out bundle — all the laying-out roles, no influence. A frame is kinematic and observer-independent precisely because influence, hence agency, is the role it excludes: a frame-process has no actor. This grounds "write not read" and PB-4 (frame-process, not observer). Orthonormality is imported (the metric, already imported via adjacency/signature); nothing is redefined.

**information = ordering + influence + flow** (= cause + flow). Information is not influence-structure in transit, but ordered influence taken up in flow, consequentially perturbing adjacency — an effect at uptake, not a constituent. It is a phenomenon, not bookkeeping. Flow enters through its clock (magnitude) projection, so physically information = cause + nonzero clock-magnitude. Flow has a single definition (the worldline process); "consequential uptake along a worldline" is **not** a second definition of flow but a **candidate** for the **rate** of flow (open) — whether uptake is flow's rate is unsettled, and PB-3 rests on it. The chain flow → mass → timelike → sub-c → no FTL holds: the photon carries influence + ordering (flow → 0), so information is constituted only where flow is present. Cause is flow-free-capable (the photon); information is not. Against current photonic usage: what a fiber link or a photonic processor transports in flight is, on this carve, influence-structure — ordering + influence in transit — and the information standard usage attributes to the photon is constituted at the flow-bearing receiver (the detector at which such protocols are, in practice, read out). No operational number changes — channel capacities and Shannon quantities are computed and measured at massive endpoints, the only regime we operate in; whether the detector is necessary rather than incidental is exactly what PB-3 stakes (below).

**gravity = ordering + influence + energy-momentum.** The third term is energy-momentum — imported from GR as-is, the declared primitive (general, not-at-rest energy-momentum), not rest-mass and not composed from the roles; rest-mass (p=0) is the degenerate Newtonian corner. Gravity composes in the non-integrable mode: ordering and scale present locally but failing to stitch globally (curvature = the loop-failure-to-close; gravitational time-dilation and length-dilation are flow-magnitude and separation-magnitude failing to globally stitch). Non-integrability is the existing not-separable mode (orientation = global orientability; arrow = fractured), not a new ingredient.

The mode lives in energy-momentum. Energy-momentum is the generator of translations (energy ↔ time-translation, momentum ↔ space-translation — conjugate to spacetime translation), and non-integrability is exactly the failure of translations to commute (loop-failure-to-close = the same two translations in different orders disagreeing); the curvature is the commutator of the covariant translation-generators. So the mode is **not** an external label on the composition — it is the commutation-structure of the energy-momentum term the framework already has. Energy-momentum (em) does not **force** non-integrability (translations can commute → flat space with em present), so the binary (flat vs curved) and the magnitude (how much curvature per em) are not fixed by em alone — that is the excluded coupling (the Einstein equation, G_N). But the mode is carried by a part (em), not missing from the parts: the framework composes the mode's **kind** (em's commutation-structure) and imports only its **value** (the coupling). This settles whether the non-integrable mode is a primitive or a GR-placeholder — it is primitive (em's commutation), with the magnitude imported.

The curvature's algebraic form reduces to three conditions, not imported wholesale:

- (a) loop-pair antisymmetry — from retrace-cancellation (a reversed loop negates);
- (b) rotation-pair antisymmetry — from metric-compatibility (transport preserves separation-magnitude → holonomy is a rotation → antisymmetric);
- (c) the first Bianchi identity (cyclic sum vanishes) — from torsion-free, which is the areal/second-order result (non-integrability ∝ enclosed area = second-order = no first-order/torsion failure; see §6.5, areal).

Pair-exchange (R_{μνρσ}=R_{ρσμν}) and the rank-4 structure follow from (a)+(b)+(c). All three are forced: (a) by retrace, (c) by areality, and (b) by metric-compatibility as a **forced** import — adjacency is defined as separation-magnitude (proper distance / area / volume, a definite integrated value), which requires transport to preserve it, since a non-metric connection makes separation path-dependent, i.e. not a magnitude, contradicting what adjacency is. That is a forced import (no first-principles freedom), not a free one. The Einstein-tensor source-form is then forced up to the ≤2-derivative truncation (Lovelock + ∇·T=0 + Bianchi); the truncation (higher-curvature terms) is the out-of-scope quantum-gravity tower, and the coupling (G_N) is excluded.

Gravity couples to energy-momentum (T: energy, momentum, pressure), not rest-mass: massless energy gravitates (light bends, light sources curvature). The coupling-target is influence-content however realized; rest-mass is only the bound-rest slice. On the metric split, the free/conformal part (Weyl — waves, tidal) is ordering non-integrability, while the sourced part (Ricci) is the field equation G=8πT — the law, out of scope (see boundary, below).

The Weyl identification [definition — the gravity composition located against GR's Weyl/Ricci split, zero confirmatory weight; excavation provenance: connects this line, the photon-conformal bullet below, the scale-free threads, and the perimeter] reads the framework's gravity as three moves onto GR's curvature decomposition: (1) **compose** the Weyl sector — ordering-non-integrability = the free/conformal curvature (Weyl), in the non-integrable mode; (2) **import** the source — T_μν operationally imported as primitive, though a partial role-decomposition of T (indices → ordering×2, pairing → separation-magnitude, plus a scalar and an unverified rank-2 remainder) is flagged and deferred (§2, energy-momentum entry); the clean split holds **while** T is imported, and if that parked decomposition succeeds, T partly enters the composition and this line moves — contingent, not settled; (3) **exclude** the law — the Einstein equation (G=8πT, the source→curvature map, G_N). So the perimeter has a GR name — compose Weyl / import T / exclude Einstein — not just a methodological stance. Of a piece (related, not identical): the framework's scale-freeness aligns with Weyl's conformal invariance — Weyl discards local scale, and the scale-setting parts are imported (T) or excluded (G_N); conformal = blind to local rescaling, scale-free = no absolute unit, so this is alignment, not equation. The photon couples to ordering only because null geodesics are conformally invariant (next paragraph) — it sees the Weyl sector. The two faces (electric/tidal, magnetic/spatial — §6.5) are the internal structure of the composed Weyl; the framework read the electric face. One object (Weyl), its faces read in the singularity section.

The photon couples to ordering only (null geodesics are conformally invariant — blind to scale); massive worldlines couple to ordering + scale (both dilations). Bending follows ordering (not a pull on mass); sourcing contributes energy-momentum (influence-content). The photon's two present components {ordering, influence} are the two gravity uses, and the absent one (flow) is the one gravity does not require for either role.

**The shared skeleton.** All compositions share ordering: cause drops flow, time drops influence, information takes all three, gravity is cause + energy-momentum. Cause is thus the shared skeleton of information and gravity — they differ only in the third term (flow vs energy-momentum).

**Boundary (composition in, coupling out).** Gravity's structure composes (ordering + influence + energy-momentum, non-integrable mode). The map from source to amount — how much non-integrability per unit energy-momentum (G_N, the field equation) — is not a term in the composition; it is the coupling relating source-term to effect-term. The framework composes the relata; SR/GR supplies the coupling. This is the same boundary as elsewhere: structural form composes, dimensionful coefficients (1/4, κ, G_N) are the adopted-around law.

**Origin of the information composition.** Information is read off the **two** corners jointly (photon flow→0, QE ordering→0), not from the photon alone. Neither corner alone shows it; information is what both lack, visible only by triangulating from both — so the definition is a two-corner construction.

This two-corner construction forces **PB-3** (photons do **not** carry information — see §16): committed before checking usage. The in-flight photon is influence-structure; information is constituted at flow-bearing uptake. The bet contradicts **standard** usage (fiber optics, starlight spectra, front velocity = c), under which photons carry information — a real collision, conceded. The claim is that standard usage conflates in-flight influence-structure with constituted-at-uptake information, and that the conflation is visible exactly at the photonic-uptake case. It is falsifiable (the same hunt as the flow-axis occupant): pre-pin "consequential uptake" operationally, then exhibit photonic logic / error-correction completing information-grade operations with **no** flow-bearing uptake. If complete information operations occur in flight, the de-conflation fails and the framework loses; if every photonic "information" operation requires a flow-bearing constitution somewhere, it holds. Status: staked, near-core, **unrun** — decided by the one test.

## 4. The write-act: mechanism and locus

Whether ordering's fate is destruction or transformation (→ influence) is open (the information paradox; companion result).

The mereology of the resultant structure (compose / missing / extra) turns on two points. (Caveat: observer-relative ordering-horizons exist without gravity — a Rindler horizon for an accelerated observer in flat spacetime — but these are removable, not invariant; the spacetime stays complete. The flow-crossing, by contrast, needs only **energy concentration** — gravity is one instance (stars), but bombs, colliders, and pair-production drive it too, so gravity is **not** required here, only over-specified. Incompleteness is expected. The stops are of two kinds: **perimeter** (couplings and laws — adopted-around or imported, not composed) and **inherited silences** (things GR itself does not fix — the arrow's direction).
