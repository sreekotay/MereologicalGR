# B3 — QES and Null Constitution

Status: working proof/contact note.  
Grade: lineage-witness for GB-3 / no-null-constitution; magnitude QG-priced, MGR-disowned.

GB-3 (A2 §8) is the parent bet: no null structure constitutes information. This note tests it against the modern black-hole entropy calculation — whether the island/QES formula forces constituted information onto null horizon structure, or instead separates area/count/cut structure from fine-grained bulk/radiation degrees and flow-bearing uptake.

## 1. Claim under test

The parent gravity bet is GB-3:

```text
GB-3:
  no null structure constitutes information
```

Expanded:

```text
not photons
not horizon null generators
not gravitational-wave null rays
```

Projection path:

```text
flow = 0
→ no constituted information
→ information requires ordered influence taken up in flow
→ null structures may transport, constrain, record, or bound
→ but they do not constitute information as null structures
```

The QES/horizon case is the sharp test because black-hole physics repeatedly invites the slogan:

```text
information is on the horizon
```

MGR's question is stricter:

```text
Does the island/QES formula force fine-grained constituted information onto null horizon generators themselves,
with no flow-bearing bulk/radiation/matter uptake?
```

If yes, GB-3 fails. If no, GB-3 survives this horizon-domain test.

## 3. Standard formula

The schematic island formula is:

```text
S(R) = min ext_X [ Area(X)/(4G_N) + S_bulk(R ∪ I_X) ]
```

Where:

```text
R
  radiation region

I_X
  island associated with candidate surface X

  quantum extremal surface / cut

Area(X)/(4G_N)
  area contribution to generalized entropy

S_bulk(R ∪ I_X)
  bulk fine-grained entropy of quantum fields on radiation plus island
```

MGR does not derive this formula. Quantum gravity supplies it. MGR asks what role each term plays relative to GB-3.

The role map is:

```text
Area(X)/(4G_N)
  the entanglement AREA-LAW term at the extremal cut: an influence-per-
  ADJACENCY balance (area = adjacency-magnitude), adjacency-side, rate

```

Important guardrail:

```text
record-like does not mean stored message.
```

The area term is record-like only in the MGR sense: count, constraint, capacity, boundary condition. It is not by itself a semantic container, a receiver, or a flow-bearing constituted item.

Likewise:

```text
S_bulk(R ∪ I_X) represents / computes fine-grained entropy of degrees.
It does not itself perform constitution.
Constitution occurs only at flow-bearing uptake/measurement at a write site (A3);
wedge reconstruction is recoverability, not constitution.
```

So the formula separates:

```text
cut / count / capacity
from
bulk fine-grained degrees
from
flow-bearing uptake
```

That separation is the whole contact.

## 5. What QES does not say

The QES is not where the information is.

The QES is not a receiver.

The QES is not a semantic container.

The QES chooses the cut.

Nor should the QES be equated automatically with a null generator. A QES need not be the event horizon and need not itself be null. The relevant point is not that the QES surface is always null. The point is that the QES / area term does not force constituted information onto horizon null generators as such. Where the structure is genuinely null, that is the framelessness fact (A0 §4): no rest frame, so no self-constitution — second-hand at a flow-frame receiver (PB-3 / GB-3, one fact).

The clean distinction:

```text
QES / area term:
  cut, count, capacity, generalized-entropy contribution

bulk/radiation degrees:
  fine-grained entropy term

flow-bearing uptake:
  constitution
```

## 6. Verdict

GB-3 stands in the QES/horizon domain.

The island formula does not localize constituted information on null horizon generators. It uses an extremized generalized entropy: an area/count term at a cut plus a bulk fine-grained entropy term over `R ∪ I_X`.

MGR reading:

```text
null horizon generators:
  may carry record-like boundary structure
  may contribute to area/count bookkeeping
  do not constitute information as null generators

QES surface:
  selects reconstruction wedge
  contributes area/count term
  is not an uptake site

bulk/radiation degrees:
  enter the fine-grained entropy term
  do not themselves perform constitution merely by appearing in S_bulk
  constitution occurs only when reconstructed, measured, or taken up by a flow-bearing system
```

So QES is not a horizon-information proof. It is a record/constitution splitter.

## 7. Forbidden residual — verdict form

The QES contact suggests a forbidden residual, not a new magnitude. An earlier draft stated it as
a magnitude-shaped pair (ε_QES-constitution = 0, ε_horizon-null-constitution = 0). **Withdrawn
(2026-07):** b4 §3 refuses exactly that move, for reasons that apply here unchanged — it re-emits
GB-3 rather than exposing anything QES-specific, it risks an absorbing re-description with no
independent test, and a magnitude-shaped ε has no equation or measurement role at this seam. The
honest statement is verdict-form:

```text
No island/QES accounting in the standard literature requires an irreducible
constituted-information term living on the QES surface or on a null horizon
generator itself.
```

This is a clarifier-grade guard, not a witness (b4 §3's grading, adopted). Meaning:

```text

  Area(X)/(4G_N),
  S_bulk(R ∪ I_X),
  radiation degrees,
  edge / gauge constraints,
  reconstruction maps,
  detector / observer uptake,
  and QG corrections,

information living on the QES surface or on a null horizon generator itself.
```

Allowed corrections:

```text
higher-curvature terms
bulk quantum corrections
edge modes / gauge constraints
replica-wormhole refinements
entanglement-wedge reconstruction maps
stretched-horizon or fuzzball-like non-null microstructure
```

Forbidden residual:

```text

```

or:

```text
S_total = Area/(4G_N) + S_bulk + S_null-horizon-constitution
```

where the extra term is irreducible to area/count, bulk entropy, radiation dynamics, reconstruction maps, detector/uptake effects, or non-null QG microstructure.

Failure condition (verdict form): a correct accounting **requiring** such a term — irreducible to
area/count, bulk entropy, radiation dynamics, reconstruction maps, detector/uptake effects, or
non-null QG microstructure — would mean the QES/horizon surface is not merely a
cut/count/capacity structure but a constituted-information site. That would break GB-3.

The standard formula does not do this.

```text

```

and the null-adjacent or extremal surface contribution in:

```text
Area(X)/(4G_N)
```

So the possible failure shape is live, but not realized by the island formula.

```text

  compatible if the degrees are timelike / near-horizon / regulator-carried,
  not null generators constituting information directly

  compatible if the microstate structure is geometric record/capacity or carried by non-null degrees
```

The reading that would break GB-3 is narrower:

```text
with no flow-bearing carrier or uptake anywhere in the loop
```

That reading was not found in the QES/island structure.

## 12. Compact result

QES is a good contact because it refuses the naive slogan:

```text
information is on the horizon
```

The island formula says something sharper:

```text
S(R) = min ext_X [ Area(X)/(4G_N) + S_bulk(R ∪ I_X) ]
```

MGR translation:

```text
QES chooses the reconstruction cut.
The area term is count / capacity / record-like boundary structure.
The bulk term computes the fine-grained entropy of radiation plus island degrees.
Constitution occurs only at flow-bearing uptake / reconstruction / measurement.
```

Therefore QES does not force information onto null horizon generators. It splits record/count/cut from constitution.

Forbidden residual, verdict form (§7; ε-notation withdrawn per b4 §3): no correct accounting
should require an irreducible constituted-information term living on the QES surface or null
horizon generator itself.
