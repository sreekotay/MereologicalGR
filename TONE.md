# TONE — MGR Writing Stance

Use this file to preserve the tone established in the A0 rewrite.

## Core Stance

- Start from the physical scene, not the abstraction. Photon first; role vocabulary second.
- Give GR full credit. GR is correct, successful, and held fixed. MGR audits what GR can successfully leave bundled.
- Treat GR as ground truth for A-notes. Other closures, such as QFT or thermodynamics, are contact closures, stress tests, or comparison regimes, not automatically ground truth in the same way.
- Respect other closures without surrendering the root stance. QFT/thermodynamics may supply standard machinery, observed facts, or guardrails, but A0's role grammar is extracted GR-first.
- When GR meets another closure, do not treat the other closure as the owner of the grammar. Ask what role-placement, guardrail, or failure condition becomes visible at the contact.

## Prose Rules

- Prefer "what becomes visible?" over "what is prohibited?" The stance is exploratory before it is proscriptive.
- Do not be overly defensive, but do be normative. State the rule, standard, or boundary plainly and let the work speak.
- Avoid meta narration. No throat-clearing openers ("this note runs…", "the claim is not that…", "A0 does not try to earn…"). No wrap-up sentences that restate what the section already showed. Show the role, rule, or consequence directly.
- Avoid too much cross-referencing. Cite the owning file or section when it prevents ambiguity, but do not make the reader chase references before the idea lands.
- Use plain terms before private terms. Say `split`, `role-bundle`, `frame transported`, `response`, `recorded`; introduce technical labels only after the idea is clear.
- Avoid circular jargon. Do not define a term by nearby terms from the same private vocabulary.
- Do not front-load caveats. Keep boundaries, but place them after the core claim has landed.
- Replace defense-brief language with audit language. The question is not "GR is missing X," but "what role was fused, imported, projected, set empty, or omitted?"
- Keep lists only when they are instruments. `compose / missing / extra`, ladder tiers, PB bets, and ledger grades are useful; long defensive inventories should be compressed.

## Voice To Preserve

- `GR-silence is not GR-prohibition.`
- `A role-gap is not a dynamics-gap.`
- `Naming is not freezing; naming is auditability.`
- `Calibration is not failure. Fitting is failure.`
- `lineage, not chronology`
- `Two doors in one wall, not one gate.`

## Conceptual Flow

- Photon opens the foundation. It shows causal ordering and effect-capacity without proper-time flow.
- Influence must then be graded. Not every consequence-capable relation is a signal, record, or information.
- Acceleration emerges only after flow has been separated. Photon separates cause from flow; acceleration asks what flow does when it remains.
- Keep "corners" as the discovery posture. MGR is clearest where roles are stripped, set empty, or forced against another closure.
- Treat success and legibility as independent axes. GR's success is why bundling can remain hidden, not evidence against the audit.

## Claims And Bets

- Chronology is in no way evidentiary. Retrodictive contact only matters when the role-route is fixed before landing and the missing/extra audit is clean.
- Retrodiction is required here, not merely permitted. QFT and thermodynamics often arrive as closures already axiomatized or formalized to fit observation; MGR therefore tests role-lineage by re-reading that fit, not by pretending to have chronological priority.
- PB bets should read as exposed commitments, not overclaims. They are the price of role-realism, not empirical victory laps.
- MGR is expected to refine as it proceeds. Narrowing, demotion, parking, or re-audit is the method working, not a rhetorical failure.
- Later-carve material should be marked as refinement, not required first-pass understanding.
- End with method, not persuasion: A0 is the root map for how claims are formed, graded, narrowed, imported, parked, or rejected.
