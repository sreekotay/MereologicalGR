## Mechanism as metering

The wave is the update; matter needs the update. A gravitational wave is adjacency re-laying
itself; matter is content that must re-bind into each next cell, and the lag is the binding cost.
The cost attaches to the **interface** — internal-bundle structure re-expressed in adjacency —
not to energy-momentum in transit: base-folding (GW self-energy) crosses no interface, so the
fabric's exemption survives to all orders, and universality and the sign-lock are structural
(everything on the bundle side pays identically; C1). What remains free is what the cost is
*metered by*: per traversed step, per adjacency-renewal, per cell content, or stochastically —
C4's family, now read as metering rules. One standing demand has jurisdiction here: b4 §6a's
PB-2.2 requires an ordering-resource to be a count, not a duration. Per-renewal metering is
duration-flavored on its face and must be count-recastable (re-layings as countable events — the
discrete reading in C5 suggests how) before any future identification with that axis could be
placed. Requirement named, not met.

## The response function

The theory object is not a number or a slope but a bounded monotone response:

    ε = f(H/H*) ,   f(0) = 0 ,   f ≤ ε_max < 1 .

f(0) = 0 discounts the constant member (response without stimulus — the EFT's placeholder,
retained only as the p → 0 landmark); boundedness *is* the wall (C4), stated as a property of f.
The working probe is the power law f = ε₀E(z)^p, with the exponent p the family's one shape
parameter.

## Registers

**Sky.** Curves through the anchor fan slowly: at z = 0.5 the span p = 0→2 is 78→102 s; at z = 1,
136→239 s, against a ~2 s per-event nuisance. Fisher, events uniform in z ∈ [0.2, 1] with
σ = 2(1+z) s: σ(p) ≈ 0.25 / 0.16 / 0.10 at N = 3 / 10 / 30. The sky measures f's local slope and
curvature over E ∈ [1, ~3].

**Early universe.** The BBN register sits at E ≈ 2.3×10¹⁷ — and thresholds in p follow
(anchor-calibrated, |κ| = ½): **visible for p > 0.77** (CMB-S4 reach), **excluded unsaturated for
0.81 < p < 0.89** (current ΔN_eff ≤ 0.3), **saturation required for p > 0.89** (the p = 1 member —
C4's T2-linear — lives here; its ε_max was the price of exact linearity). Below 0.77 the early
universe is silent and the sky carries everything. The lever arm ln E_B ≈ 40 divides *all*
ignorance: if the split is ever claimed, p is measured to ±0.03 — even factor-3 κ ignorance costs
only ±0.027 — and the claimed value cross-predicts the sky curve to ±2 s at z = 1 (≈168 s, between
the p = 0 and p = 1 landmarks and separable from both per event). One 2σ abundance anomaly, if
real, out-measures ~300 coincidences on the exponent.

**The sign, settled within the grammar (C4, κ menu).** The composite realization — n from the

as *flow* — gives κ = +½. A saturated residue therefore *adds* to BBN-side expansion, and the

**Structure covariance — the three-way sign discriminant.** Per-step metering: none. Per-renewal:

**Laboratory (candidate register only).** Many-body systems carry measured nested fronts

nature multiplies ε. Computed shortfalls: binary-pulsar Ṗ_b, 11 orders; LLR aberration residuals,

relation, two covariates, filed in the literature as different physics.

## The maximally exposed reading (b = 0)

Set the source term to zero — the invented closure removed rather than replaced — and the anchor
becomes a measurement: **ε₀ = (3.98 ± 0.30)×10⁻¹⁶** (3% timing, 7% distance), with two scales
upstream now measured, not assumed: m = M_Pl√ε₀ = 2.43×10¹¹ GeV (±4%) and H* = 5.5×10⁻³ s⁻¹.
Because the p-dependence of the curve is < 2.2% out to z = 0.1, the forecast is
**family-universal** in the entire current-detector volume:

| next coincidence at | predicted gap | anchor band | one-sided-noise scatter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 Mpc | 4.1 s | ± 0.3 | 2.7 s |
| 200 Mpc | 8.2 s | ± 0.6 | 3.8 s |
| 300 Mpc | 12.3 s | ± 0.9 | 4.6 s |
| 400 Mpc | 16.4 s | ± 1.2 | 5.3 s |

Column three is epistemic (how well the anchor fixes ε₀; the new event's distance error adds ~10%
in practice); column four is physical and conditional (the one-sided noise member's √D scatter);
their comparison is itself the discriminant. The whole family stands or dies at the next
coincidence — no exponent freedom below z ≈ 0.1.

Costs and asymmetry, priced: b = 0 requires gamma emission ≪ 1.7 s after merger, against
jet-breakout timescales of 1–2 s — the closure is swapped (prompt-at-merger mechanism), not
deleted, and GRB 170817A's spectral features would need reinterpretation. But the two readings are
not symmetric in exposure: the source reading is per-event free (b absorbs any future gap), while
b = 0 is a rigid one-parameter forecast — likelihood ratios of ~20–50× per conforming event, death
on the spot for one distant nonconforming one.

**The archive prediction (CD-8).** Combined with the pipeline audit — [−1, +5] s coincidence
windows are blind to the wedge beyond 126 Mpc; ±30 s targeted searches safe to ~760 Mpc but
association priors bury 10–20 s offsets — the b = 0 branch predicts that real counterparts of
300–400 Mpc events appear as *unassociated* short GRBs at +10–16 s. The executable test:
re-search the GBM/Swift × GWTC subthreshold archives with a distance-scaled window,
Δt ∈ [0.9, 1.1] × 4×10⁻¹⁶ · T(D). A source-side world has no reason to put gamma onsets on a
distance-proportional line; positive yield is confirmation-class, null is beaming-degenerate but
still bounds. Existing data, re-ranking cost only.
