Status: working contact note.

What B4 adds over a1 §6.4 is narrow and is the reason it exists:

```text

```

The note does **not** derive Hawking radiation, its temperature, or the Page curve. It imports standard quantities, assigns their role-location, runs the engine-eligibility test, states the observational status, and records what GB-3 already entails for this case.

## 4. The `ε_null-constitution` statement: a stipulation-consequence, not a Hawking prediction

It is tempting to write the framework's contribution as a vanishing coefficient:

```text
ε_null-constitution = 0
```

This note **declines to present that as a prediction**, for three reasons, and states it in words instead.

**(a) It is GB-3 re-emitted, not derived from Hawking physics.** ε_null = 0 follows by *definition* from GB-3 (no null structure constitutes information) together with information-requires-flow-bearing-uptake. The Hawking case does not test it; the case is shown *consistent* with it. So this is a stipulation-consistency check, not a measurement-facing forecast.

**(b) The would-be falsifier is sealed by an absorbing clause.** Any actual "null-constitution" term that showed up would, by the framework's own escape, be re-described as "reducible to area/count, bulk entropy, radiation dynamics, greybody/species, detector/uptake, or QG completion." With that clause in place, almost no candidate term can fire the falsifier. A could-fail that the construction renders nearly unfireable is not carrying predictive weight.

**(c) The `ε … = 0` notation is magnitude-theater in a framework that disowns magnitudes.** There is no equation `ε` enters, no functional it is a coefficient of, no regime where it is measured. Minting a magnitude-shaped object solely to set it to zero borrows the *form* of a vanishing-coupling prediction without its content.

The honest statement, in words:

```text
No black-hole observable in the standard accounting requires an INDEPENDENT
information-constitution term located on null horizon generators or in
null/near-null radiation transit. Constitution sits at flow-bearing exterior
uptake. This is a consequence of GB-3 applied to the black-hole case — held
provisionally, consistent with present accounting, NOT a derived new number,
and protected (hence not strongly tested) by the re-description clause above.
```

Grade of this statement: **clarifier, consistent-not-proven.** It is not a witness and is not booked as a prediction.

## 5. Observational status (the note's main increment)

The situation is asymmetric: exterior behavior is strongly constrained; evaporation, the Page curve, and the singularity are not directly observed.

```text
Exterior GR black-hole behavior:
  observed / strongly constrained.
  shadows (EHT: M87*, Sgr A*), accretion environments, mergers and ringdown
  (LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA) are broadly GR-like.
  tests exterior geometry / near-horizon boundary behavior.
  does NOT observe the singularity; does NOT observe horizon constitution.

Astrophysical Hawking radiation:
  not directly observed.
  stellar and supermassive holes are far colder than the CMB.
  primordial-black-hole evaporation searches constrain populations;
  no confirmed evaporation event.

Analogue Hawking radiation:
  reported in analogue (BEC/optical) systems.
  supports horizon / mode-mixing kinematics ONLY.
  not a gravitational test; tests no singularity, Page curve, or QES island.

Page curve / QES / islands:
  a theoretical entropy-accounting result.
  not measured for any real black hole.
  the contact is with the field's accounting, not with measured S_rad(t).

Singularity:
  unobserved; inferred as the classical GR continuation-failure boundary;
  not experimentally accessible as an interior event.
```

Disciplined read:

```text
Observed exteriors are GR-like.
No observation currently requires an information-constitution term on a horizon /
null generator. No direct observation of Hawking radiation or Page recovery
currently forces a revision of the role map.
```

So the §4 null is **unfalsified and consistent with observation, not experimentally proven** — and, per §4(b), only weakly exposed even in principle.

## 8. GB-2 versus the QES resolution — open, not sealed

Here B4 must be careful, because the loss-edge contact is where the framework most wants a witness and is least entitled to claim one.

The framework's reading places GB-2's loss edge at the **singularity** (invariant ordering-collapse), with the Page/QES correction "checking that the edge is not a permanent sink." But the modern island/QES resolution is largely a statement about the **radiation region R and the entanglement wedge**, and in many treatments the recovery is reconstructable **without reference to the singularity at all.** Two tensions follow, and the note records them as open rather than resolving them in the framework's favor:

```text
(i) Singularity-as-fifth-wheel:
    GB-2 says the singularity SOURCES the loss (classical story). The quantum
    resolution may route AROUND it (island/wedge). So Hawking does not cleanly
    WITNESS GB-2's loss-edge; it may be a case where the working accounting does
    not invoke ordering-collapse at all. GB-2 loss-edge here is a CANDIDATE
    contact, not a seal.

(ii) Invariant-loss vs cut-relative-recovery:
    the framework asserts stranding is INVARIANT (singularity), while recovery
    runs through a (cut-dependent) bipartition R / wedge. Whether the Page
    transition is invariant or cut-relative is not settled in this note; the
    asymmetry (invariant loss, cut-relative recovery) is asserted, not defended.
```

The disciplined statement is therefore: GB-2's loss edge is *consistent with* the black-hole story and *localized*, in the classical telling, at invariant ordering-collapse — but the QES resolution does not confirm it and may demote the singularity's role. The biconditional's other direction (a2: the unaccountability edge) remains unwitnessed; the biconditional remains unsealed.

## 9. Singularity / radiation split (the de-fusion that survives)

What *does* hold robustly, independent of the GB-2 question, is the de-fusion:

```text
singularity:
  where classical continuation fails; where ordering-collapse lives;
  where the loss pathology is SOURCED in the classical interior.

Hawking radiation:
  exterior channel from horizon / global-state / mode-mixing physics;
  becomes constituted only at exterior flow-bearing uptake;
  ~thermal semiclassically; T_H ∝ M^-1, P ∝ M^-2.
```

So the paradox arises because *interior ordering appears to terminate while exterior radiation appears thermal* — **not** because *the singularity emits thermal information.* "The singularity speaks" is the fusion error this note exists to block. (Mechanism language — "ordering converts into Hawking radiation" — is also barred: too physical, not derived; §11.)

  classical GR creates the stranding problem at the singularity;
  semiclassical Hawking radiation supplies the exterior record channel;
  the channel is priced externally (T_H, P, greybody/species);

Hawking radiation is not the singularity speaking. It is the exterior channel whose entropy *exposes* the singularity's ordering-collapse threat — and whose Page/QES correction shows the threat cannot be the final accounting. Whether that exposure *witnesses* GB-2, or merely co-exists with a resolution that routes around the singularity, is the open question this note refuses to close in the framework's favor.
