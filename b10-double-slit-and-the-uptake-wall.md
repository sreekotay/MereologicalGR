# B10 — Double Slit and the Uptake Wall

Status: working retrodictive-contact note.
Grade: guardrail recovery over data in hand; clarifier / verdict table; no engine move owned
(engine legs live in b8 and the D11 factorial). Magnitude QM-owned, MGR-disowned.

Detections arrive one at a time. Each electron, photon, or molecule lands at a single grain
of the screen; the fringes exist only in the ensemble (Merli–Missiroli–Pozzi 1976; Tonomura
1989). Mark one slit — one bit answers the two-way question — and the fringes vanish while
everything else stays: full flux, ~50/50 passage, single dots. The pattern becomes the sum
of two envelopes; the cross term is gone.

The experiment has two ends. This note's claim is that they are asymmetric: the slit end is
closed physics with exact imported arithmetic, and the screen end is the open seam. A century
of confusion came from auditing the wrong end.

## 5. The null guard

The unconditioned pattern's invariance under any later marker operation is the ε = 0 guard
this contact requires: a shift would put constitution in transit and break A1/A3/PB-3 in one
stroke. The guard is passed on published data — and it is QM-shared (no-signalling enforces
it too), so it earns no exposure. Its value is tier discipline: every retrocausal telling of
the eraser reads a write into the slits so that the late choice must "rewrite" it. Nothing
was written. The coincidence-gated uptake is the constitution event, and its role-route runs
through the joint state, not through a story about when the photon decided.

The recurring question — *which slit did it go through?* — is malformed at the same tier:
it requests a record-grade two-place fact in a regime where only readiness exists and no
register is present to hold the fact (A1, PB-3). Not unknowable; unposed.

## 6. Two erasers, one wall

The corpus contains two erasers, and the record/constitution wall predicts which one pays.

```text
quantum eraser:   reverses which-path readiness pre-uptake —
                  by unitary re-mixing of the marker, or by conjugate-basis
                  uptake of the marker with coincidence sorting;
                  no constituted record is destroyed; no thermodynamic floor observed

                  pays kT ln 2 — measured (Bérut et al. 2012);
```

Readiness is state structure, so it reverses for free; a record is a completed write, so its
reset draws a budget. Two doors in one wall, not one gate — and the wall was placed by A3
before this contact, so the lineage is clean. Could-fail, stated in §8: a thermodynamic
floor on pre-uptake readiness reversal, or a dissipation-free reset of constituted records,
and the wall is miscarved.

The data localize the write; they buy nothing on its ultimate character. That question exits
this note.
