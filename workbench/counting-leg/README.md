# Counting as the unfused leg, and acceleration as a count deficit

Referees: `counting_leg.py`, 14/14 (sympy, plus a 2D Poisson-sprinkling check), and
`carried_basis.py`, 6/6.

Two questions:

1. Is counting the unfused primitive under both flow and adjacency?
2. What is acceleration, as frame transport, in those terms?

---

## 1. The root carve under GR held fixed

For distinguishing spacetimes, order fixes topology and the conformal class (Malament, HKM).
The only metric data left over is one function, the volume form. In discrete form that is the
count: **metric = ordering + number.** This is theorem-grade and imported, and c5 already uses
it. But c5 assigns the leftover function to flow: *"flow → the derived measure the conformal
factor carries."*

Restricting the count to the different kinds of submanifold gives each of the corpus's peer roles:

| what is counted | across | role | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| chain along a writer's own events | timelike curve | **flow** (the writer's count) | Brightwell–Gregory; longest chain = geodesic |
| links across a cut | null hypersurface | **adjacency at its corner** (area) | Dou–Sorkin, Barton et al. |
| light out and back on one writer | ordering + one writer's count | **adjacency, spatial** (radar) | Synge chronometry (null-corner note) |

**Flow and adjacency are siblings, not roots.** They are one measure restricted to two classes
of submanifold. The corners unify as well. A null submanifold is one on which the counting
measure degenerates in exactly one direction (check 3), and whatever survives is the corner's
content:

```text
null curve          rank 0 survives    photon: ordering only, no flow       (A1)
null 2-plane        rank 1 survives    one transverse length                (no known physics role)
null hypersurface   rank 2 survives    light-sheet: area                    (null-corner note)
```

The photon corner and the light-sheet corner are the same phenomenon, one dimension apart.

## 2. Acceleration is a count deficit

Take two events on a writer, separated by the writer's own count τ. The ordering-maximal count
between them is the geodesic. For uniform acceleration (check 2a):

```text
τ_geo = (2/a) sinh(aτ/2)          this is b7 §5's sinh² interval
τ_geo − τ = a²τ³/24 + O(τ⁵)      the same leading term for circular motion (2e)
```

**So proper acceleration is how far the writer's count falls short of ordering's maximal
count.** It needs ordering, number and the writer's own events. It needs no frame, no metric and
no adjacency. The discrete check (4) gives the estimator only the causal order and the
longest-chain lengths between the writer's events in a Poisson sprinkling. It returns
**a = 0.997 ± 0.011** (true value 1.0).

Consequences for b7 and A0:

- **FT-2 gets a frame-free form.** "Acceleration = the residue of non-geodesic self-transport of
  flow" becomes "acceleration = the writer's count deficit". The deficit is generated by whatever
  selects a non-maximal chain, which is non-gravitational influence (force). Free fall produces no
  deficit, so b7's guardrail "acceleration is not gravity" holds in counting terms too. Curvature
  enters as a correction to the diamond's own volume (Gibbons–Solodukhin), not as a deficit.
- **The carried-basis face (Thomas precession) also needs no separate adjacency leg**
  (`carried_basis.py`). The space of flows is hyperbolic, and distances in it are rapidities.
  A rapidity is a count ratio: Bondi's `k = e^η` is the ratio of a receiver's count to an
  emitter's count for light pulses (check 1). A gyroscope carried around a closed velocity loop
  precesses by the loop's enclosed area. That area is fixed by **pairwise rapidities alone**:
  triangulate from the lab flow, three sides per triangle, no angles supplied. For circular
  motion it gives `2π(γ−1)` to 10⁻⁴ at v = 0.3, 0.8 and 0.99 (check 2). Integrating Fermi–Walker
  transport independently gives the same angle (check 3). The angular structure that looked like
  adjacency is encoded in the pairwise count ratios.
- **Thomas precession is not acceleration either.** Per orbit it depends only on the loop in
  flow-space (on γ), not on |a| = γ²v²/R. Two orbits at the same speed and different radii
  precess identically per loop. **Frame transport, as A0 composes it, fuses three things:** the
  count deficit (|a|), the holonomy of flow-space (Thomas/Wigner), and the diamond-count period
  (thermal, §3). Each needs only ordering, number and writers. Storage rings exhibit all three
  together: the deficit, the Thomas term in the BMT equation (tested through g−2 at ~10⁻⁷), and
  non-thermal circular noise.

## 3. Temperature is not acceleration: the double dissociation

Let `V(τ)` be the count (volume) of the causal diamond between two writer events τ apart. Checks
3b–3e:

| writer | count deficit (acceleration) | imaginary period of V(τ) (thermal) | known physics |
|---|---|---|---|
| inertial, Minkowski | no | no | — |
| uniform linear acceleration | **yes** | **yes**, 2π/a | Unruh |
| circular motion | **yes** | **no** | non-thermal circular vacuum noise (Bell–Leinaas; storage-ring polarisation) |
| geodesic in de Sitter | **no** | **yes**, 2π/H; V = (4/H²) ln cosh(Hτ/2) | Gibbons–Hawking |

**"T = ħa/2π" fuses two things:**

- the writer's count deficit, which is frame transport's forced face;
- the imaginary period of its diamond count, which exists when the writer's worldline is the
  orbit of a symmetry with a horizon, so that V depends on τ only through `e^{κτ}`.

The two coincide numerically for uniform linear acceleration, the textbook case. Circular motion
and de Sitter separate them in opposite directions. This is A0's strip test applied to the
temperature formula, and both corners are realised in known physics.

This agrees with c8 (*"the drift rate, not the acceleration, was always the variable"*), and
counting supplies a definition of that variable. **The drift rate is the imaginary period of the
writer's diamond count.** It is defined from order, number and the writer's events alone. The
thermal meaning of the period (state, KMS) stays imported, as in b7. What sharpens is b7's claim
that "the 2π is geometric": the period is already present in order and number before any field
is introduced (2d, 3b).

## 4. PB-2's gate

PB-2 is *"ordinal until a measure on ordering is earned; earn-condition = units + bound +
non-degenerate conversion rate."* Number names PB-2's ordering-resource: the count. It supplies
the **units** leg, but imported. The density ρ is a discreteness scale, and in the continuum it
is GR's volume form. **Bound** and **conversion rate** are still owed. PB-2 is not earned by
this; one of its three legs is named.

## Even accounting

| item | status |
|---|---|
| metric = ordering + number | prior art (Malament/HKM; order + number) |
| flow and adjacency as one measure on two submanifold classes | MGR re-reading; corrects c5's assignment and A0's peer listing |
| corners as one-direction degeneracies (photon, null 2-plane, light-sheet) | structural, owned; null 2-plane has no physics role yet |
| acceleration = count deficit; recovered from counts in a sprinkling | continuum: twin paradox. Discrete demo owned; causal-set-native |
| T = a/2π fuses deficit with diamond-count period; the double dissociation | **owned reading**; both dissociating corners are prior physics (Bell–Leinaas, Gibbons–Hawking) |
| c8's drift rate = imaginary period of V(τ) | owned definition; consistent with c8 |
| PB-2's units leg = count, imported | gate partially named, not earned |

**Two tests, kept separate.**

- **Relabel, in A0's sense: renaming that changes no commitment. It does not apply.** The
  commitments change in two places:
  - **acceleration:** it no longer needs a frame or a metric, only order and counts at the
    writer's events;
  - **the Unruh period:** it no longer needs a field, since it is present in order and number.
- **Export: a new claim that could fail outside GR. None yet.** Every physics consequence lands
  on occupied ground.

**What the counting derivation de-axiomatises: qualifications removed.**

- **Thermality.** "Uniformly accelerated" becomes "the writer's diamond count has an imaginary
  period". Those are writers with a horizon, which is the right domain: it includes de Sitter and
  static black-hole observers and excludes circular motion. Bisognano–Wichmann and Kay–Wald reach
  the same domain through field theory; here it comes before any field.
- **Acceleration.** "Defined through a frame (∇ along u)" becomes "a writer's count deficit
  against ordering's maximal count". It is defined wherever order, counts and writers are, in
  particular before a metric.
- **Thomas precession.** "A consequence of acceleration and boost composition" becomes "the
  holonomy of flow-space, fixed by pairwise count ratios". It is independent of |a|.

An export would have to come from where the role bookkeeping disagrees with standard practice.
The bookkeeping says which composition needs which leg; that acceleration and Thomas precession
need no adjacency and thermality needs a horizon; and that a writer's chain is selected by force.
