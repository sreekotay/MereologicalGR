# The Uptake-Toggle Design (analog Hawking)

*Workbench — experiment design, 2026-07. Grade: clarifier demonstration, and graded so by the
corpus's own engine-eligibility rule (b4 §10): the design varies the readout POVM, not causal
access at the write — T-invariance across uptake schemes is trivially quantum-mechanical, so
this cannot discriminate the framework from QM. Its value is elsewhere: the gap it fills and
the dispute it adjudicates.*

## The gap

No analog Hawking temperature has ever been measured through **discrete uptake of the
spontaneous flux**. The three platforms confound readout style with physics: BEC measures
without clicks (ensemble density-correlation estimator — pure rendering); optical fibers click
on *stimulated* classical signals; the water tank is classical throughout — and is thereby the
cleanest existing rendering exhibit: a Boltzmann ratio with no ħ, no quanta, no detection
events (Weinfurtner et al. 2011). Every published analog T is a rendering-tier object.

## The design

Metastable-helium (⁴He*) sonic horizon, Steinhauer waterfall protocol, single-atom
momentum-and-time-resolved MCP delay-line detection. One pre-registered flux (flow profile,
κ_eff, greybody/dispersion corrections fixed before data); readout toggles:

- **A** — in-situ density correlations (no discrete uptake): T_A from the correlation spectrum
  (the 2019 protocol).
- **B** — single-atom counting after Bogoliubov time-of-flight mapping: full joint P(n_H, n_P)
  per mode pair; T_B from occupations. Genuine per-quantum write (MCP threshold).
- **C** — bolometric outcoupled-beam readout: integrated flux and mean energy only; T_C via the
  1D thermal-flux form.
- **(D)** — classical seeding: T_stim from stimulated amplification ratios on the identical flow.

Predictions: T_A = T_B = T_C = T_stim within pre-computed corrections (the marginal of a
two-mode squeezed state is exactly thermal — first moments cannot see the uptake tier); the
constitutable witnesses differ by tier: only B constitutes sub-Poissonian pair statistics
(V(n_H−n_P) → 0 vs 1.84 uncorrelated-thermal at ħω = k_BT), Cauchy–Schwarz violation
(n̄(n̄+1) > n̄², ratio 2.7 at ħω = k_BT), and Bell-class witnesses; C can constitute none in
principle; marginal Fano identical (1.58) across schemes.

## The real adjudication

The Leonhardt / Isoard–Pavloff critiques of the 2019 BEC thermality claim live entirely at the
estimator tier (windowing, mode identification, evanescent channels, the zero mode). Scheme B's
click-based T carries none of those systematics: **T_B = T_A vindicates the thermality claim
against its standing critique; T_B ≠ T_A localizes the published temperature as a
rendering-estimator artifact.** Either outcome is real scientific work — the rendering/uptake
separation exercised against the literature rather than against QM.

What *would* discriminate the framework (b4 §12's demotion condition): constitution-tier facts
covarying with the rendering package while uptake is fixed — or an experiment varying causal
access at the write (partner-mode uptake behind the horizon vs exterior-only), which is the
engine-eligible subcase b4 names and this design is not.
