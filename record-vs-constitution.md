## The reduction to math

QD's diagnostics are functionals of the joint system-fragment state. The standard objects:
- partial-information curve: I(S:F) — the quantum mutual information between system S and a fragment F of the environment, as a function of fragment size f.
- redundancy: R_δ = 1/f_δ, the inverse fragment-fraction at which I(S:F) first reaches (1−δ)·H_S (the classical plateau).
- spectrum broadcast structure (SBS): the criterion that ρ_{SF} factorizes into classical-quantum form with orthogonal conditional states.

Every one of these is a functional of the state ρ_{SF}. They are computed from the density matrix — before, and independent of, any measurement.

Constitution, in the framework, is a MEASUREMENT on F — a flow-bearing uptake, a CPTP map / POVM applied to the fragment (the observer reading it). It is an operation applied to ρ_{SF}, not a number read off ρ_{SF}.

The statement:

> Constitution ∉ { functionals of ρ_{SF} }.
> Record = I(S:F)[ρ_{SF}] and the other diagnostics ARE functionals of ρ_{SF}.
> Therefore no QD diagnostic can locate, threshold, or detect the constitution event — it is not in the argument they are computed from.

This is not a claim that QD's quantities are wrong. They correctly measure RECORD — the available correlation, the structure that is broadcastable. The claim is that they cannot measure constitution, because constitution is an operation on the state, and the diagnostics are functions of the state. You cannot read an operation off its own operand.

Grade of the statement itself: definition + category fact. The framework defines constitution as an operation (the uptake); that an operation is not a functional of its operand is a category fact, not a discovery — the statement alone carries no empirical content. All the content is in the consequences and the bet below, each graded on its own.
