# C5 — Cone-Delta Bets

*Exploratory — not canon. The bet slip for [C1](c1-gw170817-propagation.md), using
[C3](c3-quantifying-the-cone-delta.md)'s estimators and [C4](c4-cone-delta-curves.md)'s theory
classes. Placed July 2026 with one event in hand (GW170817), before any second multimessenger
coincidence exists. A bet adjusted after contact is void — the adjustment is the confession.*

Each bet states what the role-carve forces that the bare EFT leaves free. That gap is the stake:
if the carve forces nothing, a confirmed cone delta is a relabel of an SME coefficient. These
seven are what it forces.

## CD-1 — The floor (headline exposure)

Constant-kernel H_p1 commits: **every future GW–EM coincidence shows Δt ≥ 1.74 s × (D / 40 Mpc)**,
within the distance error band. At 150 Mpc the gap must exceed 6.5 s; at 250 Mpc, 11 s; at
300 Mpc, 13 s. Jet physics keeps gaps at ~2 s regardless of distance. One distant coincidence
decides.

Priced expectation: **this bet loses** at the first distant event, and the loss is the product —
the propagation fusion pinned to ε ≲ 6×10⁻¹⁷ by a single observation (C3, floor statistic). What
survival would mean is calibrated by the exposure: three consecutive distant coincidences riding
the floor is the first outcome entitled to be called evidence, because nothing in the source or
selection budget (C2, carriers 1–2) grows with comoving distance.

## CD-2 — Sign lock

Fabric before content: a rider does not precede its carrier. The propagation residual, after
source modeling, **never demands matter faster than gravity — any event, any direction, any
epoch**. The fitted ε never goes negative; the wedge never inverts; anisotropy modulates the
magnitude, never the sign. (Observed EM precursors are source physics — emitted before merger —
and bill to the source carrier, not the cone.)

The EFT allows sign-flipping anisotropic coefficients; the carve does not. A single credible
negative-ε residual kills the carve outright, not just the constant realization.

## CD-3 — One matter cone

Content is content: the carve has no species slot. **Every matter-vs-matter cone comparison is
null all the way down** — photon, neutrino, any ultrarelativistic species share one cone exactly,
not approximately. A Galactic or LMC core-collapse supernova timed in neutrinos and photons lands
null at its full reach (10⁻¹⁵–10⁻¹⁶ class, C3 census); every future improvement lands null again.
In the EFT, species-blind couplings are a tuning; here they are forced, so each new precision level
is a fresh chance to die. Any measured species split, at any level, kills the carve — not the
coefficient.

## CD-4 — Pristine fabric

The delta lives in the riding, never in the fabric. **Every gravity-only propagation test stays
exactly GR**: zero in-band dispersion, zero birefringence, zero extra polarizations, GW luminosity
distance exactly the EM-inferred cosmology. Each LVK/ET/LISA upgrade is a fresh kill opportunity.
This separates the cone delta from its EFT neighbors — massive gravitons and running-Planck-mass
models tie timing anomalies to dispersion or friction; the carve forbids both. A confirmed
GW-sector propagation anomaly means the fabric itself is modified: wrong carve, banked as such.

## CD-5 — Relation, not sourced

The delta is a property of the riding relation, so its kernel lies in C4's relation-level band:
**the recovered curve is concave-to-linear in z (T1–T2 class), never convex, and residuals do not
correlate with line-of-sight structure at fixed distance.** Two ways to lose, both priced: a convex
density-class curve (T3-class separates from T1 at z ≈ 0.09 — testable in late O5), or
structure-correlated residuals (H_p2 wins the lane). Either loss banks the same boundary: the delta
would be sourced by fabric content, not constitutive of the riding — Horn-B territory with its
two-sided wall (C4).

## CD-6 — The axis

If anisotropy in cross-leg residuals is ever resolved, **its axis is the CMB dipole axis** — apex
at galactic (l, b) ≈ (264°, 48°) — not the galactic plane, not the ecliptic, not random. The
amplitude needs N ~ 10³–10⁵ events (C3, parked), but the direction costs nothing to register now
and everything to retrofit later: in the EFT the preferred direction is a free coefficient; in the
carve it is the constituting congruence, and there is only one of those.

## CD-7 — The split

Registered from C4's computation. **T1 commits to N_eff(BBN) = N_eff(CMB) exactly, forever.** T2's
early-universe residue lives on the BBN side only — ΔN_eff(BBN) = 12.3 κ ε_max, with ε decayed to
10⁻¹¹-class by recombination — so under T2 any residue appears as a *coherent BBN-vs-CMB N_eff
split*, ceiling 0.3 (current bound), CMB-S4 reach 0.06. The sorting rule this registers: an N_eff
anomaly appearing *equally* in both inferences is real dark radiation, not the cone; an anomaly on
the *CMB side only* belongs to neither T1 nor T2; only a BBN-side split is cone-delta currency.
The standard analysis currently has no label for that split — this row is where it would bill.
