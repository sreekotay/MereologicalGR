## s as interface impedance

Not a speed of anything: **a cut/conversion bound — the ratio by which influence becomes
cone-expressed or write-expressed across an interface.** Dimensionless, bounded, intensive,
configuration-dependent: PB-2's foundation form ("influence-expression per ordering-resource")
with the resource made concrete, carrying PB-2's bound-vs-realized split intact.

The kernel→carrier rung already exhibits the shape, computed: LSZ conversion with the pole residue
Z as its weight and the Källén–Lehmann decomposition as its sum rule — pole weight converts to
carrier, 1−Z stays unconverted in the continuum; Z < 1 for every interacting theory. Honesty
attached: Z's *value* is scheme- and gauge-entangled; the physical content is the pole/continuum
split. The claim is not "Z is s" but that the rung demonstrates the form. The **ratio program**
follows: conversion weights at every rung — Z (kernel→carrier, computed), distillation/heralding
efficiencies (relatedness→record, measured daily and never read this way), commit efficiencies
(record→constitution, engineered — a4's layers, with the commit cliff as a ratio at its floor).
This is also the separable answer to the relabel rebuttal: locality + unitarity explains why each
rung has a *fence*; it does not predict that the *ratios* share a grammar. Common ratio-form earns
the ladder beyond-locality status; no common form demotes the program to a relabel of standard
causality, banked as such.

  label; and GW170817's achromaticity, re-read, is existing evidence that the cone-register
