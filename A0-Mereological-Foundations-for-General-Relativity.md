## Abstract

GR is held fixed. When GR uses cause, time, frame, information, space, gravity, source, and measurement, what role-parts are bundled?

The first example is the photon. Along null propagation, proper time is zero, yet causal ordering and effect-capacity remain. Cause without flow. Cause cannot be identical with temporal flow.

Some GR-native objects are role-bundles. The metric, source, frame, tetrad, and worldline machinery do several jobs at once. MGR names the roles and tests whether the named parts compose cleanly.

The instrument is completeness, not taxonomy:

```text
compose = parts present
missing = a needed part absent
extra = present but unaccounted
```

A composition holds only when nothing is missing and nothing is extra. Naming a role, operation, projection, or composite does not make it irreducible. It makes it auditable.

## Conceit

Start with the photon. Under GR, a photon has no proper-time flow along its path, yet it still has causal ordering and effect-capacity. Something can arrive, be blocked, redirect energy, or constrain a later state. GR correctly treats that as one successful physical story. MGR reads it as a bundled role-story: ordering and influence remain while flow is stripped.

Influence is the consequence leg of cause, not one substance. The ladder keeps kernel, carrier, rendered, recorded, and constituted tiers from collapsing into one word.

The conceit stated simply: GR bundles roles that may be distinct. MGR starts from the photon, separates ordering, influence, and flow, and asks what else must follow if those roles are treated as different parts of the story.

GR's equations work by bundling ordering, influence, flow, adjacency, and energy-momentum into the metric and connection. Success and mereological legibility are independent axes.

Once flow is separated, acceleration asks what flow does when it remains: a frame is not a passive label, and influence-in-transit is not information.

MGR is clearest at degenerate corners — one role stripped, set empty, or forced against another closure.

Prime method: accept a role reading and ask what else must be true. A surviving MUST is a forced consequence the closing formalism could refuse. If nothing must follow, the reading changed no commitment anywhere, and it is relabel.

## 1. The stance: GR held fixed

MGR begins by holding GR correct as a mathematical and predictive structure. GR is a wildly successful description. MGR audits which GR roles become clearer when unfused for export, and which roles are properly left bundled.

```text
Not claimed:
  GR is wrong.
  GR needs replacement.
  MGR supplies new field equations.
  MGR quantizes gravity.
  MGR derives couplings or constants.

Claimed:
  GR's formalism may bundle separable roles.
  Those roles can be named, composed, and audited.
  GR-silence is not GR-prohibition.
  A role-gap is not a dynamics-gap.
```

GR is effective enough that it can leave roles fused without survival penalty. The equations close, predictions work, and there was never strong pressure to separate ordering, influence, flow, rendering, uptake, and constitution. Because the formalism succeeds while bundled, those fusions stayed uncontested and open to misuse — especially when GR is exported or contacted with QFT, thermodynamics, measurement, horizons, clocks, virtual processes, error correction, relational facts, and similar seams.

MGR does not replace or correct GR's dynamics. It performs a post-success audit: unfuse the roles that success made safe to leave bundled, then export the clearer grammar into domains where old bundling most easily produces slippage, over-interpretation, or conceptual misuse. The central claim is improved efficacy of application — clearer role grammar at export and contact seams — not new core field equations or central-theory scalars by default. That can sharpen how GR is applied and contacted: role-routes, forbidden remainders, and layer splits may become cleaner even when the underlying equations stay fixed. Retrodiction is required because many contact closures arrive axiom-to-fit; lineage, not chronology, is the criterion.

The stance is following GR further, not refuting it. Where GR is silent on a role question, MGR asks whether something has been left fused, imported, projected, set empty, or omitted.

At QFT contact: does a guardrail mark real tier separation on the ladder (§3), or calculational convention only?

Examples of GR-native bundling:

## 3. The influence ladder

Once influence is separated from flow, it has to be graded. Not every consequence-capable relation is a signal, record, or piece of information.

```text
bare relatedness
  → kernel influence
  → carrier influence
  → rendered influence
  → recorded influence
  → constituted information
```

`Consequence-capable relatedness` names the generic influence leg once a use has crossed from substrate into an earned influence tier. A0 keeps `influence` as the grammar's generic consequence leg in `cause = ordering + influence`, but every live use must say where it sits.

Each step adds structure. None is interchangeable with its neighbors.

```text
bare relatedness:
  connectability / comparison-possible connection of relata;
  presupposed by ordering, influence, flow, and adjacency;
  not yet consequence-capacity, not yet ordered carry, not information

kernel influence:
  coupling / propagator / interaction-term structure between sources or boundaries;
  no asymptotic carrier, no completed worldline, no flow-bearing register;
  priced by field theory as exchange kernel, not particle-in-flight

carrier influence:
  ordered influence-structure borne by a physical carrier or field mode;
  may be null (photon corner) or massive;
  flow may be stripped, reduced, or present depending on carrier;
  not constituted information while merely in transit / off write-chain

rendered influence:
  carrier or field relatedness pulled through a frame, congruence, or sampling worldline;
  response-capable under that rendering;
  still before uptake / write

recorded influence:
  proliferated ordered structure in an environmental fragment / state;
  available for uptake;
  not constitution until a flow-bearing write crosses the application threshold

constituted information:
  ordered influence taken up in flow at application consequence scale;
  information = ordering + influence + flow
```

Forbidden collapses:

```text
bare relatedness = influence
kernel = carrier
carrier = information
rendered = constituted
record = constitution
consequence-capable relatedness = any one tier by default
```

The ladder is a partial order, not a single chain. Kernel and carrier are siblings under relatedness; rendered and recorded sit on different axes (frame vs register); constitution is top only after uptake. Forbidden moves are collapses, not "skip one rung."

When QFT introduces a guardrail to block a bad physical reading, MGR asks whether that guardrail corresponds to a real role-separation or only a calculational convention. If a guardrail prices a forbidden tier collapse, it can become contact evidence at clarifier grade. If it is only bookkeeping, it does not carry witness weight.

Examples:

```text
virtual line ≠ asymptotic state / detector event     → kernel ≠ carrier
LSZ / on-shell vs off-shell                          → carrier vs kernel boundary
Unruh/KMS: thermal rendering ≠ pre-written bath      → rendered ≠ constituted
no-signaling from reduced states                     → record/render ≠ remote constitution by read alone
```

Recovering a known guardrail at clarifier grade is the expected first step, not a weakness of the audit.

## 4. Acceleration: frame-transport through flow

The photon separates cause from flow:

```text
photon:
  ordering + influence remain;
  flow is stripped

therefore:
  cause cannot require flow.
```

Once flow has been separated, acceleration asks what flow does when it remains:

```text
acceleration:
  flow remains;
  the frame-basis changes through flow

therefore:
  rendering cannot be treated as passive frame-labeling.
```

Together:

```text
photon:
  influence can exist without flow

acceleration:
  flow can change how influence is rendered before uptake

therefore:
  information is not influence-in-transit;
  information is ordered influence rendered through a transported frame
  and taken up in flow.
```

Proper acceleration is the local measurable sign of this split. It is not the whole operation. It is the clean case where a timelike worldline is not geodesic and the transported frame changes in a physically measurable way:

```text
proper acceleration:
  non-geodesic forced frame-transport on timelike flow;
  requires normalizable timelike u;
  rest mass marks the usual lab register, not the gate;
  imported handle: measures non-geodesic transport along one worldline,
  not the operation whole

frame-transport:
  invariant or operationally fixed comparison / transport of a frame-basis
  along flow, between nearby flows, or around loops;
  imported from GR's connection / curvature machinery where GR is the live sector

not counted by itself:
  arbitrary tetrad relabeling, coordinate choice, or pure basis bookkeeping
  with no changed invariant comparison, acceleration, holonomy, response, or closure fact
```

```text
Photon:
  ordering + carrier influence remain;
  flow stripped;
  no forced face on the null line; rendering + curvature retained;
  cause != flow;
  seed licenses carrier tier only (§3);
  no phase requirement at the seed.

Acceleration:
  flow remains on timelike flow;
  forced-face exhibit; frame-basis changes through flow;
  rendering != passive labeling;
  exposes rendered-influence tier (§3);
  no new influence primitive is added.
```

```text
candidate influence floor =
  phase-bearing relatedness with consequence-capacity;
  phase-structure that survives frame change
```

The diagnostic is narrower:

```text
if frame-transport changes the response,
then the sampled relatedness was at least rendered-influence
under that flow-bearing rendering.
```

So acceleration does not create influence and does not prove the full phase-bearing floor. It frame-transports rendered influence and diagnoses that tier.

This sharpens information. The photon blocks:

```text
influence in transit = information
```

Acceleration blocks the opposite simplification:

```text
information = influence + any clock
```

The missing operation is frame-conditioned rendering and uptake:

```text
same relatedness
+ different frame-transport
→ different response rendering
→ possible different uptake/write
```

So A0 keeps the composition:

```text
information = ordering + influence + flow
```

but reads it operationally as:

```text
ordered influence
→ rendered through frame
→ transported through flow
→ taken up / written
→ information
```

Gravity lives on the transport side, not the uptake side:

```text
gravity ≠ frame-transport simpliciter

gravity = ordering + influence + energy-momentum

operational chain (imported):
  energy-momentum
  → connection / curvature behavior
  → frame-transport
  → changed rendering / response conditions
  → possible uptake/write
```

Information completes on the uptake side. Gravity lives on the transport side. The bridge is not that gravity is information; it is that both share ordering + influence while diverging at transport versus uptake.

### Refinement: forced face and fixed ray

Proper acceleration is not only "massive-only." Integrated, it is the boost — the generator of inertial-frame change.

```text
forced face:
  proper acceleration along flow

integrated:
  boost / inertial-frame-change generator

sorts by:
  normalizable timelike u (flow), not rest-mass;
```

Locate the photon against that operation:

So the photon has no proper acceleration not as a bare lack but because it is the fixed ray of the operation the forced face generates — the same fact as c-invariance, read off the eigenstructure. Massive content lives in the interior of the boost flow; the photon is its asymptote.

```text

  redshift = the forced-face eigenvalue on render-only content.

massive content's rendering mixes (E, p rotate);
```

Redshift is not derived here; A0 locates its role-slot as the boost eigenvalue on render-only content. The forced face's natural horizon — the boost-Killing / Rindler horizon — is null, generated by these fixed rays. The scalar-response / Unruh run belongs outside A0.

## 5. Mereology, not taxonomy

The instrument is:

```text
compose:
  the required parts are present

missing:
  a part required for the whole is absent

extra:
  a part is present but unaccounted
```

A proposed composition is successful only when:

```text
no missing
no extra
```

Parts merely present is consistency, not composition. Taxonomy classifies things; mereology asks whether the proposed parts determine the whole.

MGR often names things that are not all the same kind:

```text
role
composite
operation
projection
grading / primitive
threshold / application scale
```

The method does not require all named entries to be ontological peers. It requires each named entry to state its type, dependencies, and grade.

Why ordering/influence/flow/adjacency and not fields/forces/particles? Because the substance vocabulary is closure-locked to GR. It cannot speak clearly to its export across seams.

## 6. Components and grammar

A0's current roster separates role, substrate, and primitive. This keeps the grammar from treating every useful word as the same kind of thing.

```text
derived roles:
  ordering:
    invariant cone-direction / causal precedence structure;
    not duration, not clock magnitude

  influence:
    grammar-leg: consequence-capable relatedness;
    live use must name a ladder tier;
    not force, push, transfer, agency, signal, or information by default;
    phase-bearing / frame-survival status belongs to later audit

  flow:
    proper-time accrual along a timelike worldline;
    temporal laying-out of relata;
    clock is its magnitude projection

imported role:
  adjacency:
    spatial laying-out / extension / nextness;
    separation is its magnitude projection;
    imported from GR's metric and named as peer of flow

substrate:
  relatedness:
    bare connectability / comparison-possible connection of relata;
    presupposed by ordering, influence, flow, and adjacency;
    not a peer-role

dimensionful primitive / grading:
  energy-momentum:
    imported from GR/SR as-is;
    dimensionful primitive;
    energy is the flow-conjugate face;
    momentum is the adjacency-conjugate face;
    mass is the rest / flow-projection face
```

Energy-momentum is not a fifth boolean axis in the role lattice. It grades cells where flow/adjacency legs provide a place for dimensionful content.

In the grammar below, `+` is not numerical addition, set union, or fusion into a new substance. It means role co-requirement: the named whole is constituted only when each listed role is present and no unaccounted role is doing the work.

```text
cause = ordering + influence

time = ordering + flow

space = ordering + adjacency

frame = ordering + flow + adjacency

information = ordering + influence + flow

gravity = ordering + influence + energy-momentum
```

These are compositions, not declarations of irreducible atoms.

Thus `information = ordering + influence + flow` means that information-grade constitution requires ordering, consequence-capable influence, and flow-bearing uptake. It does not mean those roles are homogeneous terms.

A role may later decompose. A composite may later unfuse. A named operation may reduce to a composition. Naming is not freezing; naming is auditability.

### Constraint grammar, not taxonomy

MGR is a constraint grammar: role substitutions are audited, not relabeled. When a proposed identification fails compose / missing / extra, it carries a remainder and a demotion path, not a preference for one vocabulary over another.

```text
flow ≠ ordering
influence ≠ flow
record / rendered influence ≠ constituted information
```

Subtraction exhibits:

```text
strip flow from information  → photon corner: cause without constitution
strip ordering               → influence loses directed / microcausal expression
strip influence              → flow can accrue; nothing is consequence-capable in grammar
```

The operational layer:

```text
content → rendering → frame-transport → uptake → information
```

Rendering is not uptake; frame-transport changes rendered influence without adding a new primitive; constitution requires flow-bearing write at application threshold.

One-line:

```text
MGR is not a taxonomy because role substitutions are audited:
unfused legs are co-required for named composites and mutually exclusive
under substitution; failed substitutions leave missing/extra remainders with
demotion conditions — not rearrangeable descriptors.
```

## 10. Import, calibration, and perimeter rules

MGR can use standard mathematics and observed scalars. The rule is simple: use them openly, and do not relabel imports or fitted structures as derivations.

```text
Imported machinery:
  field equations
  couplings
  standard QFT kernels
  detector response integrals
  statistical-mechanical laws
  known spectra / coefficients

Calibrated / observed scalars:
  dimensionful constants
  normalization factors
  coupling strengths
  detector gaps
  threshold scales
  empirically measured coefficients

Framework-owned if earned:
  role-route
  composition
  forced dependency
  forced scale-form
  forced dimensionless factor
  missing/extra diagnosis
  correct separation of rendering, frame-transport, uptake, and information
```

Calibration rule:

```text
Calibration is not failure.
Fitting is failure.

Allowed:
  derive the slot / form / dependency first;
  then calibrate the scalar by observation.

Not allowed:
  shop coefficients, forms, or dependencies until a phenomenon fits;
  then call the fitted structure derived.
```

This split prevents both underclaim and overclaim. A scalar may be observed; a structure must be earned.

Forced / chosen / imported / parked:

```text
forced:
  exactly one reading survives under GR held fixed

chosen:
  more than one reading survives;
  the move may be useful, but it is not forced

imported:
  accepted as part of the GR/QFT/field substrate or standard formalism
  without being derived by MGR

parked:
  live but not hunted;
  hunting would risk fitting rather than derivation
```

A presumed proscription is not a fact. Reading a GR silence as "therefore impossible" is itself a claim and must be paid for.

Import is licensed only when its status is explicit. Imported things may be used, but they do not become MGR-derived by being placed in the ledger.

## 11. Ledger discipline

The ledger keeps A0 from treating every good fit as evidence. Grades attach to claims, not objects.

```text
definition:
  a stipulation, carve, or composition;
  judged by carve-quality, carries no confirmatory weight

label:
  inert re-description;
  no weight

exhibit / seed:
  launch-site datum;
  shows the carve but cannot confirm itself

witness:
  a projection lands where it could have failed;
  carries confirmatory weight

lineage-witness:
  known guardrail recovered under retrodictive contact;
  role-route fixed before landing; missing/extra clean

probe:
  bounds an interior term without reaching the boundary;
  carries bounding weight only

echo:
  independent structure permits the reading;
  consistent-with, not evidence

clarifier:
  de-conflates or re-describes a known result while leaving standard numbers intact;
  consistency weight only, no predictive weight

failed floor:
  attempted reduction did not deliver;
  logged as negative

parked texture:
  not evidence, not denied;
  live to revisit, do-not-hunt where fitting risk is high
```

A claim's grade is ledger-indexed. It depends on the assumptions, imports, and carvings currently active.

Recovery of existing distinctions is not a weakness. When auditing a formalism that already works, clarifier and lineage-witness contacts are the necessary first step. The test is whether the unfused roles then export cleanly and reduce misuse in the contact zones.

Recovery with fixed route is the method for axiom-to-fit foundations. Relabel without prior carve is failure.

Therefore a claim should state:

```text
1. what it composes;
2. what it imports;
3. what it excludes by kind;
4. what would force re-audit;
5. what would falsify or demote it;
6. whether it is a role, composition, operation, projection, grading, or threshold.
```

A kernel-stamp means:

```text
invisible from this ledger
```

not:

```text
absent in reality
```

A ledger change reopens dependent claims. Re-audit is dependency-triggered, not calendar-triggered.

### Anti-over-unfusing and anti-over-fusing

Do not make every named seam a peer role. A named difference may be a role, but it may also be an operation, projection, threshold, or imported grading.

Use the layer test:

```text
Is it a component role?
Is it a composite of roles?
Is it an operation through a composite?
Is it a projection of imported content?
Is it a dimensionful grading?
Is it an application threshold?
```

Promote nothing to peer-role unless compose / missing / extra forces it.

The companion rule is anti-over-fusing. The opposite error is to read a shared dependency as an identity: because two roles both require a third, to treat them as one role, one gate, one operation.

```text
separability (corner-strip) test:
  to test whether two co-located roles are one,
  strip the corner where one is absent;
  if the other survives, they are separable, not identical.
  a shared dependency is not an identity.
```

Founding instances:

```text
photon:
  strip flow → ordering + influence survive → cause ≠ flow

free-fall:
  strip proper acceleration (a = 0) → flow present, uptake intact
  → uptake ≠ acceleration

scalar Unruh:
  strip orientation → flow / self-transport face survives
```

Worked instance — acceleration and uptake. Both require flow, so the massive sector renders them inseparable. But free-fall strips acceleration with uptake intact, and the photon strips flow with both gone. So acceleration and uptake are separable roles co-located only by their shared flow-dependence; the inseparability is GR's (mass supplies flow to both), not the roles'. Two doors in one wall, not one gate.

## 12. Foundation bets (PB)

The PB bets are what become exposed once the roles are treated as real composable parts rather than as convenient names. A0 states them only in foundation form. It does not cash them, grade their current evidence, or run their witness/contact history.

The bets are not optional add-ons. They are the price of treating the unfused roles as real rather than as labels.

```text
role-realism:
```
