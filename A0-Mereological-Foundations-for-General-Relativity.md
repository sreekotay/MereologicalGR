## Abstract

General relativity is held fixed as the mathematical ground. MGR does not modify, extend, quantize, or replace GR. It asks a different kind of question: when GR uses or bundles notions such as cause, time, frame, information, space, gravity-as-structure, source, and measurement, what role-parts are being composed?

The central claim of A0 is methodological: some GR-native objects are mereologically fused. The metric, source, frame, tetrad, and worldline machinery already perform multiple roles, but GR has no reason to name those roles separately because its equations work without doing so. MGR names the roles and tests whether the named parts compose cleanly.

The instrument is completeness, not taxonomy:

```text
compose = parts present
missing = a needed part absent
extra = a present part unaccounted
```

A composition holds only when nothing is missing and nothing is extra. Naming a role, operation, projection, or composite does not make it irreducible. It makes it auditable.

A0 therefore provides:

```text
1. the GR-held-fixed stance;
2. the distinction between roles, composites, operations, gradings, and thresholds;
3. the import / perimeter rules;
4. the epistemic grading ledger;
5. the current component roster;
6. the composition grammar;
7. the current open seam around rendering, uptake, and information;
8. the foundation-form PB bets implied by role-realism.
```

A0 carries no witness weight. It is the map and method by which later claims are formed, graded, narrowed, or rejected.

## 1. The stance: GR held fixed

MGR begins by holding GR correct as a mathematical and predictive structure.

```text
Not claimed:
  GR is wrong.
  GR needs replacement.
  MGR supplies new field equations.
  MGR quantizes gravity.
  MGR derives couplings or constants.

Claimed:
  GR's formalism may bundle separable roles.
  Those roles can be named, composed, and audited.
  GR-silence is not GR-prohibition.
  A role-gap is not a dynamics-gap.
```

The stance is following GR further, not refuting it. Where GR is silent on a mereological question, MGR asks whether a role has been left fused, imported, projected, or omitted.

Examples of GR-native bundling include:

```text
metric:
  ordering, adjacency, clock/separation magnitudes, signature

worldline / congruence:
  flow, frame-indexing, rendering basis

tetrad / frame:
  basis for projection, not necessarily uptake

source / T_μν:
  imported dimensionful content with flow/adjoining projections

connection:
  comparison / transport structure, later splitable by perimeter audits
```

A0 does not settle these bundles by assertion. It gives the rules for testing them.

## 2. Mereology, not taxonomy

The mereological instrument is:

```text
compose:
  the required parts are present

missing:
  a part required for the whole is absent

extra:
  a part is present but unaccounted
```

A proposed composition is successful only when:

```text
no missing
no extra
```

Parts merely present is consistency, not composition. Taxonomy classifies things; mereology asks whether the proposed parts determine the whole.

This matters because MGR often names things that are not all the same kind:

```text
role
composite
operation
projection
grating / primitive
threshold / application scale
```

The method does not require all named entries to be ontological peers. It requires each named entry to state its type, dependencies, and grade.

## 3. Forced, chosen, imported, parked

A move carries weight only if it survives the forced gate:

```text
forced:
  exactly one reading survives under GR held fixed

chosen:
  more than one reading survives;
  the move may be useful, but it is not forced

imported:
  accepted as part of the GR/QFT/field substrate or standard formalism
  without being derived by MGR

parked:
  live but not hunted;
  hunting would risk fitting rather than derivation
```

A presumed proscription is not a fact. Reading a GR silence as “therefore impossible” is itself a claim and must be paid for.

Import is licensed only when its status is explicit. Imported things may be used, but they do not become MGR-derived by being placed in the ledger.

## 4. Epistemic grades

Grades attach to claims, not objects.

```text
definition:
  a stipulation, carve, or composition;
  judged by carve-quality, carries no confirmatory weight

label:
  inert re-description;
  no weight

exhibit / seed:
  launch-site datum;
  shows the carve but cannot confirm itself

witness:
  a projection lands where it could have failed;
  carries confirmatory weight

probe:
  bounds an interior term without reaching the boundary;
  carries bounding weight only

echo:
  independent structure permits the reading;
  consistent-with, not evidence

clarifier:
  de-conflates or re-describes a known result while leaving standard numbers intact;
  consistency weight only, no predictive weight

failed floor:
  attempted reduction did not deliver;
  logged as negative

parked texture:
  not evidence, not denied;
  live to revisit, do-not-hunt where fitting risk is high
```

Discipline:

```text
Only witness confirms.
Probe bounds.
Clarifier keeps the map honest.
Echo does not support.
Failure is retained.
Uniform success is a failure signal.
```

## 5. The ledger rule

A claim's grade is ledger-indexed. It depends on the assumptions, imports, and carvings currently active.

Therefore a claim should state:

```text
1. what it composes;
2. what it imports;
3. what it excludes by kind;
4. what would force re-audit;
5. what would falsify or demote it;
6. whether it is a role, composition, operation, projection, grading, or threshold.
```

A kernel-stamp means:

```text
invisible from this ledger
```

not:

```text
absent in reality
```

A ledger change reopens dependent claims. Re-audit is dependency-triggered, not calendar-triggered.

```text
ordering:
  invariant cone-direction / causal precedence structure;
```

### 6.2 Imported role


### 6.3 Substrate

```text
relatedness:
  bare connectability / comparison-possible connection of relata;
```

Energy-momentum is not a fifth boolean axis in the role lattice. It grades cells where flow/adjoining legs provide a place for dimensionful content.

## 7. Composition grammar

The current grammar is:

```text
cause = ordering + influence

time = ordering + flow

space = ordering + adjacency

frame = ordering + flow + adjacency

information = ordering + influence + flow

gravity = ordering + influence + energy-momentum
```

These are compositions, not declarations of irreducible atoms.

A role may later decompose. A composite may later unfuse. A named operation may reduce to a composition. Naming is not freezing; naming is auditability.

## 8. Layers: roles, composites, operations, projections, gradings, thresholds

To avoid over-unfusing, A0 distinguishes layers.

### 8.1 Roles

Roles are component-functions in the composition grammar.

```text
ordering
influence
flow
adjacency
```

### 8.2 Composites

Composites are role-sums or role-compositions.

```text
cause
time
space
frame
information
gravity
```

### 8.3 Operations

Operations act through or upon roles/composites.

```text
rendering:
  content-through-frame

uptake:
  influence-into-flow

sampling:
  relatedness pulled along a flow-worldline

constitution:
  completed uptake at application consequence scale
```

Operations are not automatically peer roles. They become candidates only if compose / missing / extra leaves a remainder.

### 8.4 Projections

Projections are rendered faces of content through a basis.

```text
energy:
  flow-conjugate projection of energy-momentum

momentum:
  adjacency-conjugate projection of energy-momentum

mass:
  rest / flow-projection face of energy-momentum

temperature:
  flow-indexed response scale of accessible mixedness / relatedness
```

### 8.5 Gradings and primitives

```text
energy-momentum:
  imported primitive / dimensionful grading

couplings:
  out by kind unless specifically imported as part of the standard formalism
```

### 8.6 Thresholds

Thresholds belong to applications.

```text
record threshold:
  application-supplied scale at which uptake becomes consequential

constitution threshold:
  not fundamental scale owned by MGR;
  supplied by the record-bearing system
```

## 9. Rendering and uptake: current open seam

The present seam is the gap between content and information.

```text
content → rendering → uptake → information
```

A0's provisional grammar:

```text
content:
  phase-bearing relatedness / field structure / energy-momentum content

frame:
  ordering + flow + adjacency

rendering:
  content-through-frame

uptake:
  influence-through-flow / influence entering a flow-bearing register

information:
  ordered influence taken up in flow at application scale
```

Rendering is not uptake.

```text
rendering:
  content becomes frame-relative

uptake:
  influence enters flow

constitution:
  uptake crosses application consequence threshold
```

The reducibility question:

```text
Does rendering reduce to:
  content + frame + projection/contraction?

Does uptake reduce to:
  influence + flow + coupling + flow-bearing register + threshold?

Or does either leave a missing / extra remainder?
```

No answer is asserted in A0. The point is to locate the audit.

## 10. Mass and the content-information gap

Mass is not information and not an uptake primitive.

Current role-reading:

```text
mass:
  energy-momentum rendered into the flow-leg;
  content with a rest-frame / proper-time-bearing face
```

Mass matters because uptake requires flow-bearing capacity. A null or in-flight content can be consequence-capable without already being information. For content to become information, it must be taken up into a flow-bearing register and cross an application consequence threshold.

Candidate operational reading:

```text
mass is the usual physical mark that content has, or can enter, a flow-bearing register.
```

This is not a new primitive. It is a projection-side bridge between rendered content and uptake.

## 11. Quantum reference frames as stress test, not source

Relativistic quantum reference frames should be used as a stress test of GR-derived grammar, not as the source of the grammar.

GR-first extraction:

```text
frame = ordering + flow + adjacency
rendering = content-through-frame
```

Quantum stress test:

```text
What happens to content-through-frame when the frame-basis is itself quantum/coherent?
```

The expected pressure is not that frames gain influence qua frame. The frame-role remains write-not-read. The pressure is on rendering:

```text
classical frame-basis:
  sharp projection / contraction

quantum frame-basis:
  coherent / conditional / superposed rendering map
```

The audit question:

```text
Does rendering remain reducible to frame + content + projection
when the projector/basis is quantum?
```

A0 does not answer this. It names the seam.

## 12. Import and perimeter rules

MGR may use imported mathematics, but it must not relabel imports as derivations.

```text
Imported:
  field equations
  couplings
  standard QFT kernels
  detector response integrals
  statistical-mechanical laws
  known spectra / coefficients

Framework-owned if earned:
  role-route
  composition
  forced scale-form
  missing/extra diagnosis
  correct separation of rendering, uptake, and information
```

Example pattern:

```text
framework may derive:
  compute temperature from pulled-back relatedness through flow

standard formalism supplies:
  exact kernel, coefficient, spectrum, response integral
```

This split prevents both underclaim and overclaim.

## 13. Anti-over-unfusing rule

Do not make every named seam a peer role.

Use the layer test:

```text
Is it a component role?
Is it a composite of roles?
Is it an operation through a composite?
Is it a projection of imported content?
Is it a dimensionful grading?
Is it an application threshold?
```

Default placements:

```text
rendering:
  operation under audit

uptake:
  operation under audit

mass:
  projection / flow-face of energy-momentum

temperature:
  response scale / projection parameter

record:
  application-level stabilized state

constitution:
  completed uptake at threshold
```

Promote nothing to peer-role unless compose / missing / extra forces it.

## 14. Foundation bets (PB)

A0 states the PB bets in their foundation form because they flow from the GR-fixed role grammar plus role-realism. A0 does **not** cash them, grade their current evidence, or run their witness/contact history.

The bets are not optional add-ons. They are the price of treating the unfused roles as real rather than as labels.

```text
role-realism:
  the roles are composable constituents, not only names for equation-parts

therefore:
  projections into GR-silences become exposed claims
```

The PB bets are material because each converts a GR silence into a proscriptive or modal claim.

### PB-1 — an ordering-axis bound exists

Foundation form:

```text
The ordering/influence axis exposed by the decomposition carries a bound,
as the flow/adjacency axis carries c.
```

What PB-1 names:

```text
the existence of a bounded seam-axis
```

Not claimed here:

```text
that the bound is numerically known;
that the bound is invariant;
that any particular phenomenon has already witnessed it.
```

Could fail if:

```text
the exposed ordering/influence axis has no bound,
or role-structure is non-uniform exactly where the decomposition exposes it.
```

### PB-2 — the bound's interior is influence per ordering-resource

Foundation form:

```text
The PB-1 bound is a ratio of influence-expression per ordering-resource.
```

What PB-2 does:

```text
PB-1 names the axis;
PB-2 opens its interior:
  what the ratio is of,
  what its units mean,
  how bound and realized value can separate.
```

Not claimed here:

```text
a single scalar value for all systems;
a universal blending operation;
that interior realizations are invariant.
```

Could fail if:

```text
the ratio is not per-ordering;
it is instead per-space / per-adjacency;
or it cannot decompose into bound vs realized value.
```

### PB-3 — photons do not carry information

Foundation form:

```text
information = ordering + influence + flow

photon in flight:
  ordering + influence + adjacency
  flow → 0

therefore:
  photons carry ordered influence-structure,
  not constituted information.
```

This is not a subtraction of usefulness. It is a proscriptive composition claim: if flow is a constituent of information, then a flowless bearer cannot itself constitute information.

What PB-3 requires:

```text
flow-bearing uptake is required for information constitution.
```

Could fail if:

```text
a completed information-grade operation occurs with no flow-bearing uptake
anywhere in its constitution chain.
```

The later verification of such an operation may use ordinary detectors. That does not rescue the bet; the relevant chain is the operation's own constitution chain.

### PB-4 — information is constituted by frame-process, not observer

Foundation form:

```text
information is constituted when ordered influence is taken up in flow
at application consequence scale.
```

The bet is not that observers are absent from the world. The bet is that observerhood is not a constitutive ingredient.

Split:

```text
PB-4a rendering:
  given content/state, rendering is frame/worldline-set, not observer-set

PB-4-full constitution:
  the constitution event supervenes on the physical write-chain,
  not on observer-involvement as an extra factor
```

What PB-4 requires:

```text
write, not read;
physical uptake-chain, not observer-act;
application threshold supplied by the record-bearing system.
```

Could fail if:

```text
constitution facts covary with observer-involvement
at fixed physical write-chain;

or:

constitution facts ignore the committed physical threshold
while tracking observer-access instead.
```

### PB scope rule

```text
PB-1 / PB-2:
  architecture bets about the exposed ordering/influence axis

PB-3 / PB-4:
  information-composition bets about flow-bearing uptake and constitution
```

Gravity has no PB here. Its structure composes in the grammar; the coupling law is imported/out-by-kind.
