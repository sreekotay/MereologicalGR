## The single-order base

"You can have (P, ≺) without g, but not g without ≺" is theorem-grade: the causal order of a
distinguishing spacetime fixes the metric up to a conformal factor (Malament), and the missing
factor is exactly one function's worth of duration data — supplied by counting (order + number =
geometry). So A0's split is inherited, not conjectured: **ordering → conformal class; flow → the
derived measure the conformal factor carries**. The photon corner has a standard name in this
language: null curves are the *horismos*, the boundary relation that survives when the duration
data degenerates along the curve — cause without time, theorem-adjacent.

One convergence banked plainly: the reconstruction program's notorious open problem is recovering
*spatial* structure from (P, ≺) — and that is the corpus's one confessed gap (adjacency: imported,
parked audit). The grammar's wound and the program's wound coincide. This note inherits it; it
does not heal it.

## The pair

GR's fusion, stated exactly: one ≺, one g serving every role. H_p1's unfusion is a pair:

    (P, ≺_f, ≺_m) ,   ≺_m ⊆ ≺_f ,

two transitive irreflexive orders, matter's a subrelation of the fabric's. The lane's observables
relocate cleanly: same-leg blindness is confinement to (P, ≺_m); the cross-leg observable lives in
the **gap set** G(x) = J⁺_f(x) \ J⁺_m(x); GW170817 is one measured point of G's thickness along
one line of sight. Chains of ≺_f that fail ≺_m — fabric-timelike, matter-spacelike trajectories —
are currently uninhabited; any gravity-only sector's cone is unconstrained inside [c_m, c_f], so
"what is the dark sector's cone?" is well-posed here and nowhere else. Parked texture.

## Pair-invariants: n and ε dissolve into order data

Each order alone gives a conformal class. The pair gives more: the relative tensor g⁻¹g̃ has
eigenvalues {1−B, 1, 1, 1}, and eigenvalue *ratios* survive independent conformal rescaling of
each cone. So both the scalar ε and the distinguished direction n — the non-unit eigendirection —
are **invariants of the pair of orders**. Consequences:

- n is not equipment added to spacetime; it is the *axis of the seam*, defined exactly where the
  orders differ and degenerate where they coincide. The preferred frame exists only where the
  fusion fails, and evaporates in the GR limit. Horn A's ontology cost re-prices: there was never
  an aether field to posit — seams have normals.
- The congruence question becomes empirical: whether the order-theoretic n coincides with the CMB
  congruence or the matter-bulk congruence (C1's registered fork) is an identification to measure,
  downstream of the observable rather than upstream of the definition.
- The corpus has a precedent for seam-only structure: b4 §6b's fused limit, where the
  ordering-charge is indistinguishable from the area at stationarity and separates only under
  non-stationarity. Same shape: the pair's extra structure is visible only off the fused point.

## The pair as response cones

A physical reading of the two orders: each order is the **commutator support of its
sector's local observable algebra**. Matter fields quantized on g̃ have retarded response kernels
supported in ≺_m; fabric perturbations on g respond within ≺_f. The pair is not two abstract
orders wearing one manifold — it is two *response* cones, and the frame-entry argument (a retarded
θ is covariant only where the commutator vanishes; relativity of simultaneity supplies the
GR-space step) makes each sector's cone-guarding a covariance requirement rather than an axiom.
The coupled-sector leak — matter responding throughout ≺_f at gravitational strength — is the gap
set G(x) made operational: it is *how* anything ever measures the seam.

This sharpens the Drummond–Hathrell wall below into a single well-posed question: **does the
photon-field commutator vanish outside the metric's null cone in the D-H regime?** Support outside
means the matter response cone genuinely exceeds the metric cone there (the envelope reading);
vanishing support means the phase excursion never renders (the front reading). The wall is
unresolved, but it is now a commutator-support question, not a vocabulary dispute.

## One-sidedness

C1's sign-lock, in order form: for ≺_f to *be* the causal order, all influence lies inside it, so
≺_m ⊆ ≺_f must hold pointwise — **ε(x) ≥ 0 everywhere**. A fluctuating seam can only fluctuate
one-sided; a nonnegative field has a nonzero mean. Zero-mean "diffusive cone" readings are
ungrammatical, not merely unobserved: the noise member of the family is one-sided, floor-bearing,
and distinguishable by its second moment (C4, corrected family). The theorem is exactly as strong
as the carve it rides on (C1, Cost 0).

## The constitution cone

Flow forks with the pair. Proper time from g and from g̃ differ at O(ε) — g̃₀₀ = g₀₀(1−B) in n's
frame, the same B read on the time axis — and a trajectory in the gap set G(x) is fabric-timelike
but matter-spacelike: fabric-flow with no matter-flow. So the grammar's constitution requirement
(information = ordering + influence + flow) acquires a question the fused theory never had to
ask: **which flow?**

The grammar answers it. Write-chains are matter systems — detectors, registers, thresholds all
live on g̃ (PB-4: constitution supervenes on the physical write-chain) — so constitution is
g̃-flow-indexed and **the constitution cone is ≺_m**. Two consequences:

- **C1's re-bundling is forced, not chosen.** C1 assigns measurement and chronology to the matter
  cone and prices the bundling as adopted-for-calculation ("one event does not force that
  bundling"). Given the carve plus PB-4, it is forced: the operational "now" is defined by
  constitution events, constitution is matter-flow uptake, so operational chronology rides ≺_m.
  The upgrade is grammar-side — no observable moves (same-leg blindness applies to clocks exactly
  as to cones: a constant g/g̃ time-rate offset absorbs into units) — but one "chosen" flag in
  the lane's origin becomes a theorem of machinery the corpus already owns.

- **Chronology is protected; the paradox objection dissolves.** The standard objection to a wider
  gravity cone is causal paradox: superluminal channels plus boosts build closed loops. The loop
  machinery needs boost-invariance of the wide channel and two-sided excursions; the seam has
  neither. n is a preferred congruence, and one-sidedness (above) nests all influence inside one
  outermost cone — gap-set influence cannot close a loop. GW170817's ordering is then
  unremarkable: the GW is an early messenger *within* matter chronology, not a violation of it.

Could-fail, gravity-side (GB-3's register): a write-chain whose constitution is g-flow-indexed —
a gravitational register constituting with no matter uptake anywhere in its chain — breaks the
≺_m assignment and reopens the fork.

## The pair at a horizon

Two metrics, two trapping structures. At a black hole the gap set acquires a thickness: a shell
of order ε·r_s where matter is trapped and fabric-futures still escape — the first concrete
inhabitant of the fabric-timelike, matter-spacelike chains parked above. The horizon's fused jobs
separate there. g's horizon bounds **influence** (one-sidedness makes it *causally* outermost —
nothing consequence-capable crosses out — though radially it is the inner surface: the matter
trapping radius sits at r_s(1 + 2ε), outside it); g̃'s horizon bounds **carriers**; constitution
sites are wherever matter flow is (the constitution cone). The shell leaks influence, not
information:
escaping fabric quanta are carrier-tier influence-structure (PB-3/GB-3), and records constituted
outside from them are legal — so the information boundary is the *fabric* horizon, and the matter
horizon was only ever the carrier boundary wearing three jobs.

Temperature forks the same way, and the split's *sign* is protocol-indexed. A uniformly
accelerated detector sees per-sector KMS at T_s = ħa/2πc_s, ordering the Unruh temperatures
matter-hot: T_m/T_f = 1 + ε. Horizon temperatures order the other way: T_i ∝ κ_i ∝ c_i³ (the
matter horizon's displacement and redshift overcompensate the 1/c_i), so the Hawking split is
matter-cold, T_m/T_f ≈ 1 − 3ε. The fused reading — temperature as a property of the point —
forbids any split; the pair forces one in every protocol, with the sign carrying the protocol
(~10⁻²² K astrophysically — the register's content is consistency, not measurement). No exposure
beyond the sign lock: the fork dies with CD-2's carve — a corollary, not a bet. Realization note:
the fixed-n member cannot reach the horizon (no global unit timelike n survives there); the
composite member stays regular and delivers the split — the same member the backreaction
computation selected, and the thermodynamic audit below selects a third time.

## Discrete rigidity

Order + number needs one counting for *two* orders — volume counts points, not relations. So
(P, ≺_f, ≺_m, count) is the complete two-metric geometry with no function freedom anywhere: both
conformal factors from the same census. The two-cone hypothesis is more natural discretely than
continuously — the continuum's "one manifold, one metric tensor" is itself the assumption
≺_f = ≺_m, and the fusion under audit may be the continuum's.

The discrete setting also supplies a native metering reading: geometry *is* the order; content
*walks the links*. A per-link deficit is the per-step member; sprinkling randomness gives the
one-sided noise member. Whether per-renewal metering (the activity-tracking member) is
count-recastable — re-layings of adjacency as countable events rather than durations — is open,
and it is not idle: b4 §6a's PB-2.2 demands the ordering-resource be a count, not a duration, and
holds jurisdiction over any future identification of this structure with that axis. Requirement
named, not met.

## Form versus magnitude

The pair ansatz assumes both cones are quadratic (Lorentzian-type orders). That form is certified
by data at 10⁻³² (birefringence: one clean quadratic matter cone, not split, not
direction-dependent), while the cones' *separation* is bounded only at 10⁻¹⁶. The structure
explored here is the one the data permits at its own strictest precision: two clean nested
quadratic cones, gap unknown.
