## The axis

The corpus's seams have lived on one axis: **extent** — nested orders, magnitude deltas (ε),
width deltas (the halo). The definiteness axis is orthogonal: whether the ordering relation is
Boolean two-place, ≺(p, q), or state-indexed — ≺(p, q | branch), operator-valued, the order
entangled with a system. A mass in superposition of positions entangles the temporal order of
two events with its own branch: the relation's *arity* is raised. Not a shifted cone, not a
fuzzy cone — a cone whose definiteness has been demoted from property to relation. The delta
taxonomy completes: magnitude and width deltas are definiteness-preserving; the arity delta is
extent-preserving.

The axis has the same gauge/invariant split the extent axis showed: temporal *localizability* of
a single event is frame-relative (a clock-frame reading — the dipole's analog), while *pairwise*
order-indefiniteness is invariant under quantum coordinate changes (the quadrupole's analog).
QRF transformations are this axis's gauge group.

## The fifth job

C1 named four jobs the metric serves: propagation, measurement, dynamics, chronology. There is a
fifth, fused so deeply it goes unlisted: **definiteness-supplier**. The classical g is what makes
"spacelike" a fact — the Boolean-ness of every causal relation is a service, and every guardrail
in QFT consumes it silently: microcausality's index, the frame-entry θ (C8), the ordered
Lindblad machinery. Quantum-gravity contact is where this role unfuses from the other four. In
role grammar the QG problem is not "quantize the dynamics role"; it is that **the rendering
machinery loses its argument when the stage superposes** — the definiteness role has no owner.

## The argument ledger

*Refinement (2026-07): the fifth job, generalized.*

The two delta families differ in what they split. An extent delta splits a relation's value
structure — R(p,q) → (R_f(p,q), R_m(p,q)): same shape, two reaches. An arity delta splits the
argument structure — R(p,q) → R(p,q | α): the relation needed more arguments than the classical
description exposed. Quantum contact does not add one argument to one relation; run role by
role, every classical relation gains its own:

| role-relation | classical form | argument gained | seam status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ordering | ≺(p,q) | branch / order-control | certified, party-relative only (the switch experiments); stage-level uncertified |
| flow | τ(γ) | clock-branch — proper time along *which* arm | **open** — no clock interference at proper-time precision yet (b8's register) |
| adjacency | d(p,q) | quantum reference frame | theory-certified; no direct experiment |

| chronology | θ(x⁰−y⁰) | frame + branch | inherits ordering's status through frame-entry (c8) |

Two consequences:

**The classical metric is the universal default-argument supplier.** Each row gains a different
argument, and g fills every one with a single trivial value in one stroke: branch unique, frame
classical, order definite, clock one. The fifth job is the special case; the general statement
is that the fusion is the simultaneous discharge of every hidden argument — why the bundle held,
and why unfusing any role makes its argument reappear.

**Constitution is the one relation that runs the other way.** Every row gains an argument; the
write removes them — records are two-place Boolean facts, the one-way law's anchor. Constitution
is the grammar's arity-discharge operator: where R(p,q | α) returns to R(p,q). An arity-raisable
fabric with no discharge operator hosts no records; the reducer trilemma's three corners are the
three candidate dischargers; and the two delta axes meet at constitution because both terminate
there — the extent axis in chronometry of writes, the arity axis in the discharge.

Per-role discipline: each row carries its own existence question, settled separately —
"quantization's existence" is settled only for the influence row. The flow row is this axis's
open anchor: a clock interfered at proper-time precision would be the first per-role existence
certification outside influence — the arity analog of the extent axis's single anchor event.

## The one-way law — assembling, per-component grades

The candidate law: **definiteness is restored at classical records and never raised by them.**
Components, graded:

- *Record ⟹ separable*: classical control of order yields causally separable processes — proven
  for N ≤ 3 parties, conjectured generally; the base case is derived by construction here
  (the dephased switch is exactly the ½/½ ordered mixture). Partial theorem.
- *Coherent control cannot violate causal inequalities*: theorem, twice independently.
- *Fixed stage ⟹ fine-grained definite*: theorem (any realization in a classical spacetime
  admits a definite acyclic fine-graining; ICO with spacetime-localized systems is impossible).
- *Continuous reversible dynamics preserve order*: published, and challenged under different
  assumptions. Contested.

The law's one loophole is structural: **time-delocalized registers**. With parties delocalized
in time, even causal-inequality-violating processes become realizable in standard quantum
mechanics — so whether device-independent noncausal correlations exist in our spacetime turns
entirely on *what counts as a write site*. That is PB-4's existing question wearing
device-independent consequences: if constitution is a spacetime-localized threshold event, the
fine-grained order is definite and the loophole closes; noncausal correlations require records
that are themselves delocalized in time. Whether nature contains such records is now an
empirical question with a ledger seat.

## Party-relative versus stage-level

Every laboratory certification of indefinite causal order to date is **party-relative**: the
photonic switch fine-grains to four spacetime-localized events (each device firing at two fixed
times, hitting vacuum on the unused traversal) in a manifestly definite order, reproducing the
switch unitary exactly — constructed and verified here, zero leakage, full witness signature.
The certified indefiniteness is real and belongs to the coarse-grained party decomposition
(time-delocalized slots). **Stage-level indefiniteness — of spacetime events themselves — is
certified by nothing.** The only clean stage-level probes are gravitational: a superposed source
mass (ZCPB's temporal-order Bell test; superposed-shell switch constructions), where no fixed
background exists to fine-grain against.

## The reducer trilemma

The compelled classification. Constitution requires a definite ordering at uptake
(information = ordering + influence + flow; records are definite — the one-way law's anchor). If
the fabric is arity-raisable, the **reducer seat** must be filled; an empty seat is
ungrammatical. Three occupants, no fourth:

- **(A) The fabric reduces** — gravitational collapse (Diósi–Penrose class); constitution's
  threshold T fabric-supplied. *Bleeding*: parameter-free DP is dead (underground radiation
  searches — collapse noise makes charged matter radiate, none seen; the surviving smearing
  range no longer collapses macroscopic superpositions effectively). Ultralocal-continuous
  classical-quantum gravity is dead — by the ℓ→0 divergence intrinsic to its definition, or,
  cut off at the nuclear scale, by light-probe force noise (the corrected mechanism; the
  kilogram-scale squeeze does not close on its own). The instrument doing the killing is the CQ
  trade-off D₀D₂ ≳ D₁² — complete positivity forces any classical fabric that back-reacts to
  pay in decoherence or diffusion, floor set by the gravitational coupling, ℏ-free: **a
  conversion-cost inequality on the definiteness axis**. Forcing definiteness is never free.
- **(B) The write-chain reduces** — PB-4's assignment: definiteness at uptake via decoherence
  plus threshold; the fabric may stay superposed in transit, exactly as ε stays undefined until
  the readout cut (C1).
- **(C) No reducer** — definiteness branch-internal; records definite per branch; rendering goes
  fully relational (A0 §9's quantum-frame cell becomes machinery, not stress test).

than the thing.

## Feasibility: the Compton amplifier

Why the experimental hierarchy is what it is, in one number. Phase observables ride the Compton
frequency ω_C = mc²/ħ; order observables ride bare time. For the BMV configuration the
branch-differential dilation is ΔΦ/c² ~ 4×10⁻³⁸: the entangling *phase* is ~0.3 rad (ω_C ≈
10³⁷ rad/s does the amplifying), while the temporal-order *shift* is ~10⁻³⁷ s — twenty-two
orders below femtosecond resolution. Order-swap at fs timing needs M/r ≳ 10¹² kg/m in coherent
superposition (a ~600 m body superposed by its own scale); at ns, several times Earth's M/R.
GIE is decades away; stage-level order tests are generations away; the amplifier's absence from
timing observables is the entire difference.
