# What Could "Cause Without Time" Mean? — A Mereological Decomposition from the Photon

*Sree Kotay — working draft, June 2026.*

*Companion documents: `gravity-information-adjacency.md` (the four-momentum application) and `record-vs-constitution.md` (working derivations); see §1.5 for how refinements propagate between them.*

## Abstract

General relativity (GR) is held fixed as ground truth throughout: nothing here modifies, extends, or quantizes GR. The paper instead addresses a class of questions on which GR's mathematics is silent — mereological questions: what composes cause, time, information, space, and gravity-as-structure. The method begins at a degenerate corner GR itself exhibits. Along a null ray dτ = 0, yet cone-direction and interventional effect remain intact: the photon is causal with no proper time, so whatever cause is, temporal flow is not a constituent of it. Read at this corner, cause unfuses into three unitless roles — ordering (invariant cone-direction), influence (effect-substrate, later floored as consequence-capable phase-bearing relatedness — past force/transfer at AB, the non-abelian generalization its open hinge), and flow (proper-time accrual along a timelike worldline) — two exhibited, one stripped. The paper's single wager is to treat these roles as composable constituents and to project, under GR held fixed, what must then be true. Every step is graded: forced (exactly one reading survives) or a bet (a projection into a GR-silence, carrying a stated failure condition). Principal results: a record/constitution distinction under which information is frame-relative in the same sense as proper time and particle content (rendering witnessed at Unruh as a seam-crossing recognition, discounted by lineage; full observer-free constitution an open bet, its fails-if stated); compositions for time, space, and the invariant speed (c as adjacency-per-flow, the shared ordering-spine cancelling); de-conflations of standard puzzle-narrations (tunneling "superluminality," delayed-choice "retrocausation") with every standard number left standing; and four priced bets (PB-1–PB-4), each with its falsifier stated. Failed checks and downgrades are retained in a corrections log. Uniform success would be the failure signal.

## Conceit

The conceit stated simply: GR’s formalism bundles roles that may be mereologically distinct. This paper unfuses three of them — ordering, influence, and flow — and asks what follows if they are treated as separable constituents. (Influence, met first as effect-substrate, is later floored as consequence-capable phase-bearing relatedness — §2.) The claim is not that these roles complete GR, or replace its mathematics, but that they make explicit a structure GR leaves bundled, thereby making the theory less mereologically incomplete. Retrodiction is therefore not disqualifying by itself: the judgment rests on whether the derivation lineage is distinct from the later landing.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The question, and GR held fixed

GR is mathematically correct and its predictive compression is rightly heralded. But GR is silent on certain mereological questions, and the photon in particular is both elegant and vexing: it traverses, orders, and acts, while accruing no proper time at all. The standard response is to treat this as a curiosity of the null limit. This paper treats it instead as data — an exhibited fused composition, decomposed and performed by GR's own mathematics, of a notion (cause) that the theory uses but never anatomizes.

The framework therefore takes the mathematics and predictive compression of GR as correct a priori, and attempts to rigorously project into the questions on which it is silent: what composes cause, time, information, space, gravity-as-structure? The stance is following GR further, not refuting it. GR is sound; where it is silent or bundles roles into one object (metric, source), that is read as a mereological gap — a role not yet separated — not a foundational gap. The default on a GR silence or bundle is to separate the role, never to supply missing physics. The working expectation is that GR's regularities (e.g. a bound like c on a role-exchange) extend to separated roles.

“Cause without time” is the philosophical elision of the physics underneath: phase-bearing influence without proper-time flow, as exhibited by the photon.

### 1.2 Method

The method: from the photon under GR-fixed, unfuse exactly three unitless component-roles (no more, no fewer) — the two the corner exhibits and the one it strips. Treat those roles as composable constituents for real: this realism is the paper's declared wager, not a forced step. Then project what **must** be true under GR's mathematics if the roles compose — the compositions, the mandatory questions (e.g. the dual cell the photon's profile forces), and the bounds. The realism is paid for in falsifiable bets; the bets are the realism's price, and their failure conditions are stated where they are made.

The mereological instrument is completeness — compose / missing / extra — not taxonomy:

- **compose** — parts present.
- **missing** — a part needed but absent.
- **extra** — present but unaccounted.

A composition holds only with no missing and no extra — the parts determine the whole, nothing left free. Parts merely present is consistency, not composition.

**Forced** is the gate the method runs under: a move carries weight only if no alternative reading survives once GR is held fixed as ground-truth math — exactly one reading live. If two or more remain, the move is chosen, not forced (manufacture) — a move declined under this methodology. The compose / missing / extra checks test whether a decomposition is complete; forced tests whether the step was necessary. Completeness can pass and still be taxonomy — only forced makes it carry weight.

Import and proscription run under the same gate. Import is licensed by forced-from-ground + completeness, never by being GR-native (a non-GR thing imports if it derives forced from GR + method ground and enters complete; only the unforced is excluded). A presumed proscription — reading a GR-silence as a prohibition — discharges as a bet, not a fact. Outside-GR is not disqualifying; bare exclusion is not a move.

The method and measures (compose / missing / extra) are tools for reducing epistemic incompleteness, not a claim that any decomposition here is complete or its parts irreducible.

### 1.3 Claims and non-claims

What the paper claims: a set of role-separations read off GR's own corners; compositions built from them (time, space, c) graded individually; contact-grade readings of standard phenomena in which every standard number is left standing and only the narration changes; and four bets — projections into GR-silences — each priced with a falsifier. What it does not claim: no new dynamics (the field equations and couplings are imported or out by kind); no derivation of magnitudes as GR is scale-free (dimensional counts are presently open, couplings are out by kind); no substance-ontology (the components are roles, not stuff — §2); and no adjudication among interpretations of quantum mechanics beyond the specific role-content checked. Where the framework's reading merely re-describes, the re-description is labeled as carrying no weight (§1.5).

### 1.4 Relation to existing work

Individuation by role is fundamental physics' default mode, not an exotic posture: GR ontologizes gravity as laying-out structure rather than substance; Wigner's classification individuates particles as representation-profiles; gauge fields enter as connection coefficients, individuated by how they transform and couple. The mathematics has no further slot for what anything is beyond its role-profile. What is distinctive here is the procedure — individuation by corner-degeneration (what survives when GR's own math strips a constituent to zero) rather than by invariance — and the grading discipline attached to it. Adjacent programs are noted rather than adjudicated: relational quantum mechanics and the thermal-time program operate at the same constitution and flow seams (a consistency run against Frauchiger–Renner-type pressure is run in the working document — passed, on pre-FR commitments; promotion pending its gate); the quantum-Darwinism criteria literature is the live testing ground for the record/constitution boundary (reduced and tracked in the working document). Where the framework's readings collide with named formalisms, the collisions are run as checks and logged, in both directions.

### 1.5 Epistemic grading (labels — applied per claim, not per object)

- **definition** — a stipulation or composition (a carve). Judged by carve-quality, not truth; carries **no** confirmatory weight - that work follows. Most of the framework's construction is definitions (the compositions, the roles, the perimeter).
- **label** — inert re-description of one thing in the framework's vocabulary. No weight; rare.
- **exhibit (seed)** — the datum at a projection's launch site: the corner a carve was read off. Zero collision-room at the site itself (the carve fits it by construction), so it can hold no confirmatory weight; its value is exhibition — the decomposition shown — and what it forces downstream. The photon corner is the framework's exhibit; the weight is carried by the independent landings (QE onward).
- **witness** — phenomenon run through compose/missing/extra; could have failed mereologically, didn't (or failed correctly). Carries confirmatory weight.
- **probe** — interior/non-extremal reading that **bounds** a term without reaching its boundary. Carries bounding weight only.
- **echo** — independent structure our reading maps onto; **silent** (permits, doesn't force). Carries **no** weight; consistent-with, not evidence.
- **clarifier** — a contact-grade verdict: the framework's reading re-describes or de-conflates a known result correctly, predicting no new number (the numbers are owned by the standard formalism). Confirms consistency, carries no predictive weight. Distinct from echo (a clarifier is the framework's own reading checked against a phenomenon; an echo is an independent structure that happens to permit the reading).
- **failed floor** — attempted reduction that didn't deliver; logged as negative.
- **parked texture** — not precluded, not suggested; demoted from evidence, live to revisit; do-not-hunt where hunting is the fitting trap.

**Discipline**: only witness confirms; i.e. probe bounds; echo / failed-floor / parked carry no confirmatory weight and may **not** be cited as support. This attempts to keep broad exploration from collapsing into "everything confirms me." An object can hold different labels in different contexts (neutrino = witness mereologically, probe on the s-axis).

**Landing-site rule** (the discipline's general form): every check tests a projection — seed + imports + derivation steps — never the bare seed; what a pass confirms is graded by where the projection *lands*, not where it launches. (i) Landing = launch site (the output restates the seed in another register): zero collision-room; a pass verifies the translation and nothing more — exhibit-grade ("the seed cannot witness itself" is this endpoint). (ii) Independent landing, already resolved when the projection was forced: witness-by-retrodiction — chronology does not disqualify (Mercury's perihelion is the canonical license); the weight is gated by the use/lineage audit (the confirmer must not have been an input to the construction). (iii) Independent landing, unresolved: a bet — the later check produces the witness. The grade-assignments encode this: bets are reserved for live exposure; resolved independent landings enter as witnesses with lineage doing the anti-tuning work; launch sites hold neither. (Three distinct zero-weight failure modes follow, and they must not be conflated: tautology — could not have been false, a failure of *form*; retrofit — tuned to fit the confirmer, a failure of *use*; identity-landing — the premise offered back as its own evidence, a failure of *independence*. Retrodiction is none of these: known-early is not used-in-construction.)

**Status-scope**: this doc asserts only what its own derivations establish; its status lines (open / owed / parked) are relative to that basis. Refinements derived in the working doc (record-vs-constitution.md) supersede them only on promotion, after a cold read in a later session; until then, the working doc holds the set's current state of those items, and this doc's statuses are correct at their own level, not stale.

Descriptive move-names (excavation, reflection, quant-composition) name **how** a move was made or its shape; they are not weight-classes and never substitute for an epistemic slot. Every claim carries a slot from the list above (definition / label / witness / probe / echo / failed-floor / parked) regardless of how it was reached. A composition — including a quant-composition — is a definition (zero confirmatory weight).

## 2. Components

These are role-names, not substance-claims. Mereology gives the parts and their separability; it is silent on what the parts are made of. "Component" therefore means separable role, not object — which is why entries of different ontological grade (a role, a substrate, a bookkeeping projection, a dimensionful primitive) sit together in one list, sorted by role rather than by kind of being.

The entries are not all of one grade, and the grading is the point. The framework's own carve yields exactly **three roles — ordering, influence, and flow** (§1.2: the two the photon corner exhibits, the one it strips). These are the specific new components: cause, time, and information compose from them, and the bets price them. The remaining entries support the three and arrive differently. **Adjacency** is a role of the same kind (unitless, laying-out) but of different provenance — imported whole from GR's metric rather than unfused at a corner: the spatial peer of flow, needed for space and c, named late. **Relatedness** is the substrate all four presuppose. **Energy-momentum** is the dimensionful primitive, imported as-is. So the count is three derived roles, plus one imported role, a substrate, and a primitive — not four undifferentiated roles; derived-at-corner versus imported is the import discipline of §1.2, holding at the roster itself. Some entries were used pervasively before being listed — energy-momentum, adjacency, signature, frame — because GR bundles them into its metric, source, and tetrad; they were named late. Naming a bundled role is the mereological move: the entries below are GR's roles separated, plus influence, which is separated at the QT seam.

The carve sits clean against the existing compositions, each layer adding one thing: relatedness = bare connection; **influence = consequence-capable phase-bearing relatedness**; cause = ordering + influence; information = ordering + influence + flow. The sharp form: *influence is relatedness whose coherent/phase-like structure can become consequential under an allowed uptake; information is that influence written onto flow.* The carve gives influence a floor without making it force, magnitude, signal, or already-information.

**The dimensionful primitive and its contingency.** Declaring the dimensionful primitive carries a contingency: the framework stands on SR/GR kinematics. It adopts SR/GR's dimensionful primitive (energy-momentum, via E=mc²); it does not adopt SR/GR's dynamical law (the field equations, G_N). Mass is the rest-frame face (energy at p=0); energy-momentum is the general, not-at-rest primitive. **Mass as flow-projection** (emerges from reading space and gravity together; it completes the relationship, it does not derive the magnitude). Momentum is the conjugate of adjacency (space = ordering + adjacency); energy is the conjugate of flow (time = ordering + flow). Rest-mass — energy at p=0 — is therefore energy-momentum lying purely in the flow-conjugate face, with the adjacency-conjugate (momentum) zeroed: mass is the flow-projection (temporal alignment) of energy-momentum, and momentum is its adjacency-projection (spatial alignment). E=mc² (rest, pure flow) and E=pc (massless, pure adjacency) are the two limit-projections; intermediate systems split across both. The magnitude relation (m²c⁴) is imported (the dispersion relation); the role-alignment (mass↔flow, momentum↔adjacency) is the framework's reading. This makes one quantity — energy-momentum — projected onto the two laying-out roles, and accounts in one move for three otherwise-separate facts: rest-mass anchors flow (having mass = energy-momentum aligned with flow = proper time accrues); massless means flow→0 (energy-momentum all in the adjacency face); and gravity couples to energy-momentum, not rest-mass (it couples to both faces; rest-mass is only the flow-face slice). Not a truth-claim — a relationship the mereology completes.

**The law.** The law (couplings: G_N) is the metric on this space, not a region in it. Cell-occupancy is the open work; the structure carries no confirmatory weight.

**Flow is physics-silent beyond its magnitude.** Physics reads only the clock (magnitude) representation of flow, never a "becoming" gloss. This is confirmed at the hardest case: the Page curve is a static S(τ) — a magnitude — plus ordering (the curve's orientation). So "information = cause + flow" enters physically as cause + nonzero clock-magnitude.

**Flow as frame phenomenon (write, not read).** Proper time accrues along a timelike worldline — frame-intrinsic, observer-independent. "Write" names this process: the act with no actor (the worldline accrues; no agent is involved). "Read" smuggles an observer. This grounds PB-4 — information constituted by frame-process, not observer (see §16, Predictions). The write's mechanism, its strict scope, and the located result it yields are given in §4.

## 3. Compositions

Each composition below is a definition — a carve, judged by carve-quality, not truth, and carrying no confirmatory weight (§1.5). They are written as role-sums; "+" means the roles are jointly present and determine the whole, with no missing part and no extra (§1.2). The compositions share one skeleton — ordering — and differ in what they add to it.

**cause = ordering + influence.** No clock, no flow; scale-robust; no remainder. This is the seed composition, read off the photon corner: what survives when flow is stripped. In expanded floor-language (stated once, then abbreviated): **cause = ordering + consequence-capable phase-bearing relatedness**, and correspondingly **information = ordering + consequence-capable phase-bearing relatedness + flow**. The short forms (with "influence") below are these with the floor folded back in.

**time = ordering + flow.** No influence. The clock is fractured by scale (quantum: no time operator; cosmic: a(t)). Flow enters through its clock (magnitude) projection; the process beyond its magnitude is physics-silent (§2).

**space = ordering + adjacency.** The spatial peer of time — adjacency (extension / nextness) where time has flow (the worldline process). Adjacency enters through its separation-magnitude projection (proper distance / area / volume). This restores a symmetry the framework was missing: it had time-as-composition but no space-as-composition (space was a degenerate corner). "True space" is ordering + adjacency, not a leftover.

The conjugates follow the two laying-out roles: energy is conjugate to flow (time), momentum conjugate to adjacency (space) — the two faces of the energy-momentum primitive. [Reading clause: these are role-conjugacies — dynamical/Fourier duality — not operator-pairs. Under an operator-pair reading the table collides with Pauli's theorem (energy bounded below denies a self-adjoint conjugate time operator, while momentum, unbounded, gets its x); the table survives only as dynamical/Fourier duality, the operator machinery imported and asymmetric between the pairs, the asymmetry owned by the spectrum. A forced specification of which reading is tenable, not a kill. This bullet is the canonical statement of the conjugate layer; the gravity document (doc-2 §9) points here and keeps only its gravity-specific uses.] Mass is **not** a third conjugate: it is the invariant magnitude of the four-momentum (its rest value, energy at p=0), not a quantity conjugate to ordering. The four-momentum's two legs are time-momentum — the (ordering+flow)-projection, the timelike part, with magnitude from flow and zero when flow→0 (which is why the photon is massless) — and space-momentum, the (ordering+adjacency)-projection, the spacelike part, ordinary momentum. So energy-momentum = time-momentum + space-momentum, where "+" again means "projects into": the role-decomposition names the two projections but does **not** specify how they combine. The combination law — the quadrature — is not separately imported but a theorem of imports already licensed [re-graded; matches doc-2 §7]: the metric (imported with adjacency/signature, metric-compatibility already a forced import) takes its norm on the leg-decomposition, and that norm is the mass-shell E²=(pc)²+(mc²)² — the quadrature of the two legs, with mass the invariant magnitude. Single-counting: the metric and signature remain the imports; the quadrature is their consequence, not an extra law. (Photon: pure space-momentum, time-momentum zero, E=|p|c. Rest: pure time-momentum, space-momentum zero, E=mc².)

Ordering has no conjugate quantity, and this is a **category fact**, not a gap. Ordering is the shared spine of both legs — it appears in both time = ordering+flow and space = ordering+adjacency — the axis the legs are projected **along**, not one of the projected legs; asking for its conjugate quantity is a category error, like asking the momentum of a coordinate axis. This is why ordering cancels in c's units: c = space/time = (ordering+adjacency)/(ordering+flow) = adjacency/flow measured along the shared ordering spine — leg over leg, spine cancelling. c is the rate at which the two legs trade along the spine; the photon is the spine made manifest (the null ray, the cone-edge), c saturated. Contrast s = influence/ordering, a ratio measured **against** the spine, with ordering in the denominator and not cancelling — which is why c and s are not exact peers: c is leg/leg along the spine, s is influence against it. And ordering and influence are conjuncts (cause = their conjunction; they degrade separately into sequence and correlation), not conjugates — they do not trade off — and ordering, being the spine and not a leg, cannot host a leg-shell at all: the ordering axis carries a ratio-bound (s) but no mass-shell. The c/s asymmetry, grounded.

This is decomposed GR, round-tripping: composing the role-projections back gives GR's dispersion relation (the four-momentum, its two legs, the invariant mass) with nothing missing and nothing extra. The decomposition is faithful — a consistency result, not a new claim.

**frame = ordering + flow + adjacency** (= space ∪ time, glued on shared ordering; GR's tetrad, decomposed). The influence-free laying-out bundle — all the laying-out roles, no influence. A frame is kinematic and observer-independent precisely because influence, hence agency, is the role it excludes: a frame-process has no actor. This grounds "write not read" and PB-4 (frame-process, not observer). Orthonormality is imported (the metric, already imported via adjacency/signature); nothing is redefined.

**information = ordering + influence + flow** (= cause + flow). Information is not influence-structure in transit, but ordered influence taken up in flow, consequentially perturbing adjacency — an effect at uptake, not a constituent. It is a phenomenon, not bookkeeping. Flow enters through its clock (magnitude) projection, so physically information = cause + nonzero clock-magnitude. Flow has a single definition (the worldline process); "consequential uptake along a worldline" is **not** a second definition of flow but a **candidate** for the **rate** of flow (open) — whether uptake is flow's rate is unsettled, and PB-3 rests on it. The chain flow → mass → timelike → sub-c → no FTL holds: the photon carries influence + ordering (flow → 0), so information is constituted only where flow is present. ## 7. Influence / information

The relation of influence to information runs parallel to that of mass to weight: **influence : information :: mass : weight.** Influence is invariant and carrier-side (like mass), present even in massless carriers (the photon, QE); information is a frame-relative rendering, massive-receiver-side (like weight). The two are conflated because we **are** the massive frame rendering it, and the language — "signal," "carries a bit," "channel" — encodes the conflation.

**No FTL**, two ways. Constructively: information = ordering + influence + flow, and flow present → mass → sub-c (the chain runs through mass / clock-magnitude, the read projection, not through any process beyond magnitude, which is physics-silent). By dissolution: what is "instantaneous" (QE) or "at c" (the photon) is **influence**, not c-bound, and it is mistaken for information (which is c-bound); un-conflated, influence may be instantaneous or at-c while information is constituted only at sub-c massive endpoints, never across the gap. On the axis: c = space/time is a rate and bounds information (propagating); "FTL influence" is a type-error, since influence has no rate (no per-time), and s = influence per ordering-**resource** is per-ordering, not per-time — not a rate, so no contradiction.

**The c/s symmetry** is on the temporal axis, not the spatial one. c = space per flow-resource caps the space crossed per unit flow/time, and the photon saturates it as flow → 0; s = influence per ordering-resource is **temporal**, single-system (influence correlated with itself as ordering varies), with QE the ordering→0 corner. In the interior it is **not** a scalar dilution law but **response-shaped**: influence-expression = f(ordering-density, system spectrum, access/environment spectrum). Ordering does not simply dilute; it **samples** — and depending on what it samples (mass-splitting, bath spectrum, boundary modes) influence-expression is suppressed, enhanced, or redirected. This splits the condition. ### 11.5 Quantum Zeno [probe on s] — predict-before-check

Prediction (committed before sources): (1) Zeno = dynamical s-axis — more ordering-resource (measurement frequency) suppresses evolution (transition hindered, system held near initial state); same direction as LG (more ordering → less quantum expression); (2) scaling should rhyme with LG's quadratic; (3) could-disappoint: anti-Zeno (more measurement accelerating decay) — if generic, "ordering dilutes" breaks.

Sources: Misra-Sudarshan; Facchi-Ligabò arXiv 1702.04284; Murch (Zeno/anti-Zeno) arXiv 1703.08371; single-atom QZE arXiv 2509.24438.

Check — partial; confirms direction **and** bounds generality:
- Zeno regime: frequent measurement suppresses evolution; survival → 1 as measurement frequency rises. More ordering-resource → evolution/transition hindered (less quantum expression). Same direction as LG. ✓
- scaling rhymes: short-time survival is **quadratic**; LG excess advantage ~1/n². Same shape (quadratic in ordering-resource), not same quantity.
- ties to write-not-read: Zeno = suppression by ordering-dense interaction/registration (works via environment, stochastic fields — not only measurement). Confirms ordering-resource = registration-density, not observation.
- anti-Zeno **bounds** generality and supplies the mechanism: in open/bath-coupled settings more measurement can **suppress** or **enhance** decay per the Kofman-Kurizki universal formula (Nature 405, 546, 2000) — the modified rate = overlap of measurement-induced broadening (∝ measurement rate) with the reservoir spectrum. QZE when broadening ≫ reservoir width; AZE when narrower. So ordering-density does not simply dilute — it samples the environment spectrum.
- the bound: LG dilution is the **clean**/closed limit (no reservoir to sample → only broadening → monotone). The **open** interior is response-shaped (KK overlap). s-axis interior = response function, not scalar law; the clean monotone is its environment-free special case.

Findings update (s-blended terms): each of neutrino, LG, and Zeno/KK bounds its own s-blended term lawfully. They do **not** converge on a shared scalar (refuted), LG is not reducible to the others (no reservoir), and one fixed blending operation does **not** generate all three (computed: KK native, neutrino needs coherent-not-intensity, LG needs a hand-supplied spectrum — the operations differ in kind). So s is a name over three differently-generated bounded terms, not a unification — parked, not disproven. No number predicted; clarifier.

A break-attempt was run adversarially — try to find a **third** way ordering acts on influence, neither suppress nor grant. On the "two-mode sign" framing (a parked candidate of the framework's own) the result is **refuted**: the KK mechanism shows ordering acts by **sampling** (overlap of measurement-broadening with the reservoir spectrum), and the sign (Zeno = suppress, anti-Zeno = enhance) is set by **what is sampled**, not by an intrinsic mode of ordering — suppress/enhance are two outcomes of one operation (sampling), spectrum-set, not two fixed-sign modes. Since this refutes a claim the framework actually parked, it is a real negative. The relocation re-confirms held content with no new claim: Zeno/anti-Zeno is the **interior** of the ordering-axis (sampling a reservoir, spectrum-relative), while the boundary (QE, ordering→0, nothing to sample) is where the bound lives, and LG is the featureless-spectrum interior limit — consistent with "interior = response function, boundary = bound." There is no damage to PB-1/2: PB-2 uses "influence" as the role (the floor openly unsettled) and never claims univocity, and that coherence-loss and dissipation are distinct (dephasing ≠ decay) is textbook, not a hit on any claim.

This states a general tool: run the uncollapsed roles as a simultaneous system; it **closes** iff the combining operation is **structural** (selection, threshold, order — role-native) and **fails** iff the combining operation is **quantitative** (a rate/balance the roles cannot generate). QES closes to the extremal form (combining = selection, structural; short only the coupling); Zeno does not close (combining = overlap-rate, quantitative, imported whole). Closure is binary, and it sorts exactly along the structural/quantitative-relations line.

### 11.6 Casimir [failed-floor / does not advance] — influence as constraint?

Prediction (committed before checking): influence = boundary-conditioned mode constraint, with geometry knob F/A = −π²ℏc/240a⁴ (ideal plates, geometry-only). could-fail: if it reduces cleanly to local force between fluctuating charges (no independent boundary/mode role), it re-opens "influence = force."

The could-fail **fired**. The could-have-failed of a composition **is** its missing/extra check, and counts as a seal **only** if committed before the confirmer is in context (a retrospective "could have come out wrong," stated after the answer is known, is not a seal). Deriving "we **need** exactly these parts" is a seal; relabeling the given parts is not.
- directional bias: overclaims pull toward whatever reads as the currently-valorized move — confirmation → performed unification; licensed damage → performed collapse; valorized self-correction → performed damage against a manufactured strawman. The brake must be indifferent to which direction reads well. The check is the same regardless of direction: does the claim (connection, damage, or correction) attach to something actually committed before the confirmer was in context? Connection-bias, damage-bias, and correction-bias are one bias.
- each leap must be **earned** (committed under risk, before the confirmer, attaching to prior claimed structure). Real claims earn each leap → load-bearing → damage against them is informative. Phantom claims (inserted whole, unearned) break for free and fit for free → neither damage nor confirmation moves anything. Performed-connection = an unearned leap dressed as derivation; performed-damage = a hit on a leap never taken. Same fault, opposite faces. Damage is fine — only against a claim that earned its leaps.
- probe as node-finder: walking a derivation, each stall (reach for a composition not yet built) names a node to fill — problem-specific, unlike the full static gap-graph (which over-generates). Some nodes have outsized depth (a stall opening a sub-structure). QES named adjacency (retrodicted), step-7 commensuration, step-5 selection.
- corner vs regime: the conversion-corners (see §12.2 (Parked)) are role-**lack** corners (flow→0, ordering→0) where a conversion is pure. Role-**rich** regimes are the contrast — e.g. the recombination plasma wall is ordering-rich and uptake-dense, **not** a corner; its collective behavior (radiation pressure) is consequential-uptake at dense scale. Consequential uptake has a scale axis: sparse end (discrete uptake, form-clean) → dense end (coupled medium). The corners are where conversions show pure; the role-rich regimes are the application-space where the scale is set.
- where this can fail: holding GR-math as ground-truth is a choice of what to fix, not a claim it is correct — so the framework can be wrong three ways. (a) Derivation error: a step claimed forced when an alternative reading in fact survives — the exposure is sharpest at the forced steps, which is also where it is most checkable. (b) An error in our handling of the GR we fixed. (c) The world disagrees: GR-math itself fails where we leaned on it, or a bet's falsifier fires. The framework inherits GR's empirical fate (c) and adds the two internal ones (a, b) — a bounded exposure, located by the very choice to hold GR fixed.
