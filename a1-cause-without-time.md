# A1 — Cause Without Time

*Working draft, June 2026.*

Status: application document / photon-run of A0.  
Primary target: A0 photon seed, composition grammar, and PB-1 through PB-4.  
Grade: seed-run + projection ledger. Not a new dynamics, not a replacement for GR/SR/QFT.

Under GR held fixed, a photon follows a null geodesic with `dτ = 0`, yet it can be emitted, blocked, redirected, absorbed, and later constrain a physical state. Causal ordering and effect-capacity remain while proper-time flow is stripped. So cause cannot require proper-time flow.

```text
The photon exhibits cause without proper-time flow.
```

The photon seed therefore unfuses:

```text
cause = ordering + influence

time = ordering + flow
```

A1 runs that seed. It asks what follows if ordering, influence, and flow are treated as separable role-parts rather than fused under ordinary temporal language.

---

## 1. What the photon seed shows

The photon under GR gives one clean corner:

```text
null propagation:
  dτ = 0

yet:
  cone-direction remains;
  causal ordering remains;
  effect-capacity remains;
  later uptake can occur at a detector or absorber.
```

Thus:

```text
ordering remains
influence remains
flow is stripped
```

That is the seed. It does not carry the framework's weight by itself. It shows that one ordinary bundle is not atomic:

```text
cause ≠ temporal flow
```

This is the first role-exposed case in the corpus. Role-exposed does not mean ontologically bare; it means stripped enough that one constituent role can be seen carrying work. The photon strips flow while leaving ordering and influence. Later notes use the same pattern at other corners: one role is stripped, set empty, or forced against a contact closure so the remaining role-work becomes visible.

A role-exposed read is licensed only when:

```text
1. a normally bundled role is silent, stripped, or held fixed by the setup;
2. a remaining role carries a concrete computation, scale, guardrail, or burden;
3. the standard machinery that supplies numbers or dynamics is named.
```

The phrase “cause without time” means:

```text
ordering + influence
without proper-time flow.
```

It does **not** mean no ordering, no relativity, no geometry, no change, or no later detector time.

## 2. What the seed does not show

The photon seed is a launch-site carve, not a witness for itself.

It does not by itself derive:

```text
adjacency;
relatedness;
energy-momentum;
the full influence floor;
QFT state machinery;
new field equations;
new constants or couplings.
```

Those enter differently:

```text
adjacency:
  imported from GR's metric as spatial laying-out / flow's peer

relatedness:
  substrate presupposed by ordering, influence, flow, and adjacency

energy-momentum:
  dimensionful primitive imported from GR/SR

influence floor:
  later carved as consequence-capable phase-bearing relatedness,
  not licensed by the photon alone
```

So A1's discipline is:

```text
seed gives the defusion;
downstream landings carry any weight.
```

The seed directly separates three roles:

```text
ordering:
  invariant cone-direction / causal precedence structure;
  not duration

influence:
  grammar-leg: consequence-capable relatedness;

  clock magnitude is its projection
```

The core result:

```text
cause = ordering + influence

time = ordering + flow
```

This is not taxonomy for its own sake. It explains why a null carrier can be causal while lacking proper-time flow.

## 4. Composition consequences

Once the seed separates ordering, influence, and flow, the immediate composition grammar is:

```text
cause = ordering + influence

time = ordering + flow

information = ordering + influence + flow
```

The photon in flight has:

```text
ordering + influence + adjacency
flow → 0
```

Adjacency rides the null carrier (the photon propagates in space); it is not part of the information composition, so flow remains the missing leg. Therefore:

```text
photon in flight:
  ordered influence-structure
  not constituted information
```

This is PB-3 in its sharp form.

The detector or absorber can still constitute information because it supplies the missing flow-bearing uptake:

```text
photon transit:
  influence-structure

detector uptake:
  influence enters flow-bearing register

record threshold crossed:
  constituted information for the application
```

So A1 keeps transit and constitution separate:

```text
carry ≠ constitute
render ≠ uptake
record ≠ in-flight possession
```

## 5. Space, frame, and adjacency

Adjacency is not derived by the photon seed. It is imported from GR's metric as the spatial peer of flow.

```text
space = ordering + adjacency

frame = ordering + flow + adjacency
```

A frame is not a mind or observer. It is the physical/rendering structure in which content can be projected:

```text
frame:
  ordering + flow + adjacency

rendering:
  content-through-frame
```

A1 therefore treats observer language cautiously. The physics sits in frame/worldline/rendering/uptake, not in observer psychology.

## 6. Energy, mass, and null transit

Energy-momentum is imported from GR/SR.

Role-facing:

```text
energy:
  flow-conjugate face

momentum:
  adjacency-conjugate face

mass:
  rest / flow-projection face of energy-momentum
```

Photon:

```text
m = 0
E = |p|c
```

A photon has no rest frame and no proper-time flow, but it has energy-momentum rendered relative to a flow-bearing worldline.

Thus photon energy is not a one-place in-flight constituted property. It is rendered at a worldline:

```text
E = -p · u
```

This dissolves redshift-style pseudo-problems: there is no in-flight flow-bearing register from which energy drains. There are different renderings by different worldlines.

## 8. PB-3: photons carry influence-structure, not information

PB-3 follows directly from the composition grammar:

```text
information = ordering + influence + flow

photon in flight:
  ordering + influence + adjacency
  flow → 0
```

Therefore:

```text
photons do not constitute information in flight.
```

They can carry structured influence. They can be modulated, absorbed, blocked, counted, and used in communication systems. But constitution occurs when the influence is taken up in a flow-bearing register.

Framelessness is the frame-level face of the same fact: with no flow the photon instantiates no frame (A0 §4), so it is rendered and constituted only second-hand — at the receiving flow-frame, never in flight. The flow-route and the frame-route are one constraint, not two.

Failure condition:

```text
a completed information-grade operation occurs with no flow-bearing uptake anywhere in its constitution chain.
```

Ordinary detector verification does not rescue the photon-in-flight as the constitution site; it confirms that uptake happened later.

## 9. PB-4: constitution is write-chain, not observer act

PB-4 says:

```text
information is constituted when ordered influence is taken up in flow
at application consequence scale, with threshold rule T pinned before contact.
```

Observerhood is not an extra constitutive ingredient; constitution supervenes on the physical write-chain at a pinned threshold.

The split:

```text
rendering:
  content becomes frame/worldline-relative

uptake:
  influence enters a flow-bearing register

constitution:
  uptake crosses application consequence threshold T (pinned before contact)
```

So the live question is not:

```text
who observes it?
```

but:

```text
where is the write-chain completed?
```

This is the bridge to record/constitution diagnostics: environmental record is structured influence; constituted information requires uptake/write.

## 10. Contact cases

A1 uses contact cases only where they test or clarify the seed-run. They do not replace the derivation.

### 10.1 Redshift

```text
E = -p · u
```

Energy is rendered at a worldline. Redshift is a comparison between emission/uptake renderings, not an in-flight energy-drain mechanism.

Grade:

```text
clarifier
```

### 10.2 Quantum entanglement

Entanglement can show relatedness without ordinary spatial adjacency. It pressures naive locality language but does not by itself derive influence's full floor.

Grade:

```text
pressure-test / clarifier;
not a free witness for the whole framework
```

### 10.3 Neutrino mass

Neutrinos are useful because tiny mass means tiny but nonzero flow-bearing capacity.

Role reading:

```text
massless limit:
  flow stripped

massive tiny limit:
  flow present but small
```

This probes the null/timelike boundary without supplying a new constant.

### 10.4 Horizons and singularities

Horizons test access and rendering. Singularities test continuation and ordering-collapse.

Disciplined split:

```text
horizon:
  access boundary / rendering restriction

singularity:
  candidate invariant ordering-collapse
```

Observer-relative horizons do not automatically count as ordering-collapse.

### 10.5 Aharonov-Bohm / phase-bearing influence

AB-style holonomy motivates the phrase:

```text
phase-bearing relatedness
```

but the abelian case does not settle the non-abelian influence floor. It is a useful face, not the full floor.
