## The induction

Every adjacent pair of fronts ever measured against its envelope is strictly nested: constitution
inside signal (commit cliffs), signal inside correlation, correlation inside the Lieb–Robinson
ceiling, LR inside c — and, one level up in abstraction, Tsirelson's realized correlation bound
(2√2) strictly inside the no-signaling envelope (4), unsaturated for no known reason. The only
adjacent pair assumed *exactly coincident* is the outermost gravitational one — measured once
(C1). Strict interiority is the observed norm; exact coincidence is the exception claim. The
inner nestings have known mechanisms, so the induction transfers only where a mechanism slot
exists — but the pattern re-prices the reflex prior in both this seam and C1's.

## The hypothesis

A third order: entanglement-establishment is not instantaneous but finite-superluminal — a
relation ≺_q with cone speed v_q in a frame n, with c remaining the limit of *spacetime and
information* (the matter cone bounds metric propagation and constituted signal; the establishment
tier may ride wider). Existing bounds: v_q ≳ 10⁴ c from long-baseline Bell tests with moving
analyzers — experiments that are, methodologically, cross-frame timing tests of a preferred-frame
cone, whose results are bounds filed as confirmations of infinity. Since v_q ≫ 1 + ε, the stack
would extend far *above* the fabric cone: ≺_m ⊆ ≺_f ⊂ ≺_q.

## The forced classification

Two established facts close a fork in the grammar. Bancal–Pironio–Gisin: any finite-speed
influence reproducing quantum correlations within its cone enables genuine signaling in
multipartite configurations. Data: no signaling, ever. Now classify establishment as *influence*:
finite v_q → signaling → excluded; infinite v_q → influence outside every ordering envelope →
`cause = ordering + influence` fails structurally. Both branches dead. Therefore:

    entanglement-establishment sits below the influence tier — relatedness —
    or the grammar is wrong.

The ladder's tier assignment here is compelled, not chosen — a surviving MUST in A0's own sense,
falsifiable in place (any controllable consequence through entanglement kills it; a standing
forever-null of CD-3's species). Promotable toward the core-grammar table if it survives
scrutiny; carried here at seam grade. The stakes of misclassification are total: were
establishment influence, ordering's envelope migrates to ≺_q and both metric cones become
interior structure — the relatedness/influence line is carrying causality itself.

## The escape and the gate

Finite v_q evades the theorem only one way: correlations must *degrade* near the ≺_q boundary in
exactly the manner that protects no-signaling at the constituted tier. So the scenario does not
merely permit the ladder — it requires the relatedness→influence tier gate as load-bearing
physics, and it generates the first placeable observable the constitution layer has ever had:
boundary degradation, at a computable location.

## Boundary windows and the sidereal search

A pair at baseline L sits outside a v_q cone only when measured within τ = L/v_q of n-frame
simultaneity:

| baseline | v_q = 10⁴c | 10⁶c | 10⁸c |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geneva-class (18 km) | 6 ns | 60 ps | 0.6 ps |
| Micius-class (1200 km) | 400 ns | 4 ns | 40 ps |
| GEO-diameter | 23 μs | 233 ns | 2.3 ns |
| Earth–Moon | 128 μs | 1.3 μs | 13 ns |

The frontier scales linearly with baseline: a lunar Bell test reaches v_q ~ 10⁸ c with
tens-of-nanoseconds timing. The signature is archival and distinctive: **sidereal-periodic
correlation dips**, twice daily, as Earth's rotation sweeps the pair through n-frame
simultaneity — searchable in existing satellite entanglement and QKD archives at re-ranking cost.
And the congruence fork (C1) bites concretely: the two candidate apexes (CMB vs matter-bulk, 28°
apart) generate *different dip templates*; the search runs both, and a detection selects the
tine — tying this seam's n to the gravitational seam's n through pure geometry.
